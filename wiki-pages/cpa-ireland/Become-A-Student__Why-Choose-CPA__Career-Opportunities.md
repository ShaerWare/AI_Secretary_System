## Career Opportunities

Many career opportunities exist with the CPA qualification. Students and members work in practice and industry and abroad.

#### JobSearch

CPA Ireland jobs board houses open accountancy and finance roles for trainee, qualified and part qualified accountants across Ireland. You can also join CPA Irelands Talent pool

#### CPA Irelands online careers portal

24/7 access to instructional videos and training materials in CV Preparation,Interview Preparation, Interview techniques and practice, Building your brand, Networking and  CPA Trainee Podcast series
