# CCU Higher Education Scholarship

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland students can now apply to be in with a chance to win a €500 bursary towards their CPA studies.

Applications can be submitted online; you will need proof of study and be a member of Capital Credit Union.

The closing date for applications is the 19th of October at 5pm.

CPA Ireland student has an excellent chance of winning and if your not in, you cant win. The prize will be selected at random from all entries.

Find out more on the Capital Credit Union website here: https://capitalcu.ie/higher-education-scholarship/
