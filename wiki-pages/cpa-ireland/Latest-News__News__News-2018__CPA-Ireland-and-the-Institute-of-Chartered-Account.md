# CPA Ireland and the Institute of Chartered Accountants India renew Mutual Recognition Agreement

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

The Institute of Certified Public Accountants in Ireland (CPA Ireland) and the Institute of Chartered Accountants India (ICA India), entered into a mutual recognition agreement (MRA) in October 2010 and this agreement was renewed again on October 5th, 2018 at CPA Ireland headquarters in Dublin.

The agreement offers qualified members of each body to become a member of the other body, and to enjoy the benefits that each organisation offers.

With over 300,000 members and 1,000,000 students, the Institute of Chartered Accountants India have a global reach with MRAs with the South African Institute of Chartered Accountants, The Canadian Institute of Chartered Accountants and CPA Australia, to name but a few.

Further information on this MRA can be found here. http://www.cpaireland.ie/about-us/cpa-international/international-agreements/ica-india
