# Quality Assurance Process

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

If you receive a notice for a quality assurance review in 2024 you will have received a date for the review of your firm’s engagement files. This may include both audit and non-audit files. This review may be conducted onsite at your offices or in a remote fashion. Such arrangements will be made with you in advance with details set out in your notice.
