# CPA Ireland Sponsorship Award with Munster Technological University (MTU) - Kerry

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland was delighted to sponsor the award for the highest results across the four modules: Advanced Management Accounting, Income Tax & VAT, Financial Statement Analysis, and Advanced Financial Reporting on the Bachelor of Business (Honours) in Accounting, Munster Technological University Kerry.

Congratulations to Laura O’Driscoll for your hard work and great achievement.

Pictured left to right:
Kristina Murphy (CPA Ireland), Peter Ring (Lecturer), Jude Condell (CPA Ireland), Raymond O Connor Desmond (Head of Department Business; Hotel, Culinary Arts & Tourism), Laura O’Driscoll (Prize Winner), Simon Quinn (Lecturer)
