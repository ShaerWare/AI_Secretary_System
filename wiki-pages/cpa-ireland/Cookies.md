### What level of cookies does CPA Ireland use?

| Cookie Level | Corresponding API Level | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No cookies | System | Enables the minimum cookies required for the website to work, and remembers whether the user allows cookies or not. |
| Only essential cookies | Essential | Enables all cookies required for website functionality, but no tracking cookies. |
| All cookies | All | Enables all cookies used on the website. |

### Third Party Cookies

CPA Ireland uses remarketing services to advertise on Third Party websites to you after you have visited our website. We and our third party vendors (Google, Meta, Microsoft, LinkedIn) use cookies to inform, optimize and serve ads based on your past visits to our service.

These cookies allow these third party vendors to identify what you find popular, allowing a more personalised and relevant selection of advertisements to be displayed when you browse the internet. Information collected about your browsing activity on this site may result in you seeing ads for CPA Ireland across other sites on the internet.

If you wish to opt out of third party advertising cookies, there are some steps you can take:

Google

To opt out of Googles use of cookies or device identifiers you can visit Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively you can opt out of third party vendor use of cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page or control the use of device identifiers by using your device’s settings.

You can find out more about Meta’s use of cookies and how to opt out of their cookies here.
