# CPA Ireland is delighted to announce that the Digitalisation Hub will be accessible by all.

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

The Digitalisation Hub highlights the many digital solutions currently available in tax, payroll, audit, advisory and much more.
 
We encourage everyone to take the time to navigate around the hub and see what’s on offer. It can be accessed at www.cpaireland.ie/hub
 
Eamonn Siggins, CEO CPA Ireland “Collaboration is fundamental to progress in our profession and with that in mind, CPA Ireland is delighted to open up access to our Digitalisation Hub and share this resource with our accounting colleagues around the world and to the businesses they serve. I encourage accountants everywhere to take the video tour of the Hub and see the benefits that it can offer you and the businesses you serve.”

Manuel Arias, IFAC – "One of the greatest parts of my work is bringing exciting initiatives led by IFAC member organizations to a global audience. CPA Ireland's Digital Journey Experience.  A fantastic initiative by CPA Ireland. "
