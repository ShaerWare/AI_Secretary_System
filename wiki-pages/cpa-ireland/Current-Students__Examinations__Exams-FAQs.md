# Examinations FAQs

Answers to frequently asked questions on Examinations.

- When can I sit examinations? Strategic Level Examinations are held twice annually in April and August of each year.
- How do I apply for examinations? Students must apply for examinations via their MyCPA Dashboard which can be found here http://portal.cpaireland.ie/login.aspx
- How long do I have to pass the examinations? A student that first registered as a student with the Institute at the Foundation Level must pass all levels of the Institute’s examinations within a maximum of 9 years.
	A student that first registered as a student with the Institute at the Professional Level must pass all remaining levels of the Institute’s examinations within a maximum of 7 years.
	A student that first registered as a student with the Institute at the Strategic Level must pass this level of the Institute’s examinations within a maximum of three years.
- How many exams can I sit at each diet of examinations? Students are permitted to sit a maximum of 4 examinations per exam diet.
- How do I receive my exam results? Examination Results are only available via your student My CPA  dashboard.  A full breakdown of the detailed marks by question is given along with the overall result.  Results are published approximately 6 weeks from the last day of examinations.
