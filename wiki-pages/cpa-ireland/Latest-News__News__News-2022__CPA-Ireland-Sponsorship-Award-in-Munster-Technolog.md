# CPA Ireland Sponsor Award in Munster Technological University Cork

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland were delighted to sponsor the award for best student on the Bachelor of Business (honours) in Accounting (Part Time) at Munster Technological University Cork. The winner was Brendan Keniry and it was presented on behalf of CPA Ireland by Patrick Kearney, CPA Ireland member.

Pictured from left - right:
Dr. Áine Ní Shé - Registrar & Vice President for Academic Affairs, Dr. Claire O‘Sullivan Rochford - Head of Accounting & Information Systems Department, Brendan Keniry - Awardee, Patrick Kearney - CPA Ireland Member, Mr. Gerard O’ Donovan - Head of Faculty of Business & Humanities and Dr. Breda Kenny - Head of School of Business.
