# Women in Business 2019 - Balance for Business

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland, with thanks to the kind sponsorship of AIB, hosted a special event on Women in Business yesterday evening. The event provided female professionals the opportunity to share leadership strategies, build their own networks and hear from successful female leaders and entrepreneurs. A second Women in Business event will take place in Cork on Wednesday June 19th.

The evening was a huge success, speakers included Rosie Fitzgerald, Creative Director, BBC iPlayer, Katie Daughen, Head of Brexit Policy, British Irish Chamber of Commerce and Michell Mc Greal, Head of Diversity and Inclusion, AIB.

For further details and to register for our Cork Women in Business Event on June 19th, visit https://cpaireland.ie/CPD/Conferences/Women-in-Business-2019
