# Why Data Analytics for Finance?

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

> Method: The course will be delivered through livestreamed lectures once a week, with class notes available in advance. Students will have the option of attending the livestreamed sessions or watching them at a later date. Students will have the option to take a mock exam in advance of the April diet of examinations.
> Location: Online via Chartered Accountants Ireland Learning Hub. Once you are enrolled you will receive access to the Learning Hub before the course starts.
> Time: Every Monday at 6:00PM.
> Dates: 13 October 2025 - 23 March 2026.
> Cost: €500.00

- The course will cover the underlying mathematical concepts and technology involved in Data Analytics, and will focus on the application of these concepts in the domain of Business and Finance.
- The tutor will use tools such as Excel RStudio
- Students should have a base knowledge of Excel when starting this course.
