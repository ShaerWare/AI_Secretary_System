# Key Dates

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

JANUARY 2026
14 January - Registration opens for April 2026 Strategic Level examinations (via MyCPA).
31 January - Deadline for submission of 2025 Training Records (via MyCPA)

FEBRUARY 2026
6 February – Application deadline for admission to membership (March Oversight Board)

MARCH 2026
3 March -  Deadline for all registered students to apply for the April 2026 Strategic Level examinations.

APRIL 2026
3 April - Application deadline for admission to membership (May Oversight Board)
24 April to 1 May - Strategic Level Examinations.  A detailed timetable can be found here.
30 April  - Submission date for Q1 2026 Training Records (via MyCPA)

MAY 2026
8 May -  Application deadline for admission to membership (June Oversight Board)

JUNE 2026
12 June - April 2026 Examination Results issued.

JULY 2026
1 July - Registration opens for August 2026 Strategic Level examinations (via MyCPA).
31 July - Submission date for Q2 2026 Training Records (via MyCPA)
31 July - Application deadline for admission to membership (September Oversight Board)

AUGUST 2026
4 August - Deadline for students to apply for August 2026 Strategic Level examinations.
21 August to 28 August 2026  - Strategic Level Examinations.  A detailed timetable for the August 2026 examinations can be found here.

OCTOBER 2026
1 October - Application deadline for admission to membership (November Oversight Board)
9 October - August 2026 Strategic Examination Results issued.
31 October - Submission date for Q3 2026 Training Records

NOVEMBER 2026
6 November - Application deadline for admission to membership (December Oversight Board)
