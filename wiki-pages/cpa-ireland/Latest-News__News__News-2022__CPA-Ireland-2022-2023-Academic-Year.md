# CPA Ireland 2022/2023 Academic Year

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

The 2022/2023 academic year will run from September 1st, 2022 - August 31st, 2023. Exams will take place in April and August 2023 and students can sit between 1- 4 exams per exam sitting. If you are interested in registering and starting your studies, get in touch with CPA to confirm your exemptions and starting point into the qualification.

Apply for Exemptions

If you hold any prior qualifications completed in the last 10 years you may receive exemptions from the exams. Please email a copy of each year of your result transcripts from all qualifications to hello@cpaireland.ie

Starting from the Foundation Stage
If you do not have any prior qualifications and are above the age of 23, you can start from the Foundation Stage. If you are below the age of 23 and have no qualifications, CPA will need to review your Leaving Certificate results to confirm you meet the minimum requirements. Please email a copy of each year of your Leaving Cert result to hello@cpaireland.ie

CPA Student Registration
After CPA have confirm your exemptions and starting point, you can then register as a student. The requirements and steps how to register can be found here.

Study Options
Registered students will have the option of studying with one of the approved CPA Educators by online lectures or part-time evening lectures that will be commencing mid-September. A list of the CPA Educators can be found here.

Fees & Key Dates
A breakdown of the fees to CPA and the Educators and some key dates can be found here.
