# Amalgamation Information

In February 2024, members of Chartered Accountants Ireland and CPA Ireland elected to join together as a single professional body. 
This process commenced on the 1st of September 2024 and ultimately all of CPA’s members, students, operations, and services will be incorporated into those of Chartered Accountants Ireland.

## Payment of Subs for 2026

Please note that for those members who joined Chartered Accountants Ireland on 01 September 2024 as a consequence of the amalgamation payment of subs for 2026 must still be made through your profile accessed through this website. Please do so by logging into your member profile and completing the payment process.
Find out more here

The due date for payment of all subs is 30 April 2026. Members who have not paid their fees by this date will have a late fee of €50 automatically applied to their account.
 

For queries relating to your fees please contact Sabrina Moore – Sabrina.Moore@charteredaccountants.ie Phone: 01 2030 453

## Integrating legacy CPA systems with Institute systems

Work is now underway to bring all member and firm records together into a common system and website.

A dedicated project team has been established. Over the coming months, CPA legacy member and firm details will be migrated to the Chartered Accountants Ireland systems.

Once complete, all members and firms will access their accounts through the Chartered Accountants Ireland website.

More detailed information on the process and next steps will be shared with members and firms in the coming months.
Work is progressing well, and the project is on track to finish in the second half of 2026.
