# New National Accounting Competition for Third Level Students

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

### About the competition

This new competition which will commence on 14 February 2022 will feature weekly sets of accountancy theory questions.

- 10 Week Competition
- Hosted on Quitch, an award-winning assessment platform
- Weekly prizes for all participating students
- Overall winners of the competition announced at the end of April
- All assessments on Quitch will be based on first year accounting material
- Engagement with the competition will help students to develop and test their basic accounting knowledge
- Students can gauge their progress against other students
- Healthy competition and fun

Grainne Oates (Founder of Quitch) originally from Sligo but now based in Australia has run this competition successfully in Australia and New Zealand over the past few years and is now offering the same fun learning opportunity to Irish students.

This has been made possible through collaboration with CPA Ireland and the Irish Accounting and Finance Association in the form of a nationwide competition among accountancy college students.
