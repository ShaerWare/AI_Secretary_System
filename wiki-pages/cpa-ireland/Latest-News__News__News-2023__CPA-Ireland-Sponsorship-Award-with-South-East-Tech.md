# CPA Ireland Sponsorship Award with South East Technological University Carlow

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland were delighted to sponsor the award for the winning team from the Strategic Analysis Module on the Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Accounting Year 3 in South East Technological University Carlow.

Congratulations to Emma Cummins, Tara Jacob and Katie Maguire for your excellent work and great achievement.

Pictured left to right:

Dr. Martin Meagher (Head of Department of Business), Tracy Byrne (Lecturer & Programme Director, BA (Hons) in Accounting), Adam O'Reilly (CPA Ireland), Emma Cummins (Prize Winner), Tara Jacob (Prize Winner), Katie Maguire (Prize Winner), Jude Condell (CPA Ireland), Dr. Ed Dennehy – (Lecturer in Business Management)
