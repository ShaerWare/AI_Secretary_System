# CPA Ireland Approved Educators for 2025/26

Following the Amalgamation with Chartered Accountants Ireland on 1 September 2024, our CPA Approved Educators will no longer offer courses for Foundation and Professional level as the final sitting for these exams was in August 2024. 

Students who are eligible to sit Strategic Level examinations in 2026 should contact their chosen educator to start examinaton preparation for Strategic Level subjects.
