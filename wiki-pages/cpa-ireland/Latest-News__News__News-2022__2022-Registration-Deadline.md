# 2022 Registration Deadline

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

The 2022 deadline to register as a student with CPA Ireland is 1 December 2022. If you’re not a registered student with CPA Ireland before this date, you will not be eligible to sit exams in April 2023.
 
Please see the information below on how to register as a student with CPA Ireland. 
   
Step 1. Confirm Exemptions
If you have any prior qualifications, you may be eligible for exemptions. Please email a copy of each year of your result transcripts to hello@cpaireland.ie and we will confirm the exemptions you can receive.
   
Step 2. Register as a CPA Student
As part of the registration process, certain documentation will be required to complete your registration along with payment for the pro rata 2022/2023 student subscription fee. A full list of requirements and the registration form can be found here.
  
Flexible Study Options
CPA Ireland work with award winning educators who provide our students with best-in-class professional accountancy education, with both live and online recorded lectures. Start your studies anytime to exams. A list of our approved educators can be found here.    
 
Fees   
A full and transparent list of the fees to CPA and the educators can be found here.
