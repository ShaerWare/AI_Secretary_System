# 2018 Pay and File Income Tax Deadline - ROS Problems

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

#### Update: ROS Extension Sunday 17th November 2019

Revenue have advised that the ROS filing deadline has been extended to midnight Sunday 17th November 2019 in light of the difficulties with the ROS system during the week.

_____________________________________________________
13/11/2019
As previously advised, the 2018 Pay and File Income Tax Deadline was extended to 6pm today, 13th November 2019. We are aware that there continues to be problems with the system again today. Revenue have advised that the ROS system will be unavailable until 1pm today.  They have also advised that in light of the difficulties experienced with the ROS system over the last couple of days, a surcharge will not apply to any returns filed this week, up until midnight on Sunday 17 November.
 
In order to over-ride the Revenue’s Income Tax Surcharge when making a return the following should be applied;
 
1. Remove Surcharge from Column B
2. Agree with Revenue’s calculation [you are only agreeing with calculation to line (c)(ii) only]
3. Adjust Balance of Tax Payable / Tax Overpaid to reflect no surcharge in Column B
4. Enter note to say why the surcharge has been deleted.
 
We will continue to keep you updated.
