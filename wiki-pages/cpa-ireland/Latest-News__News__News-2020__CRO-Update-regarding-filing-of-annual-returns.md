# CRO announce extension to 30 September 2020 Annual Returns Dates

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland welcomes the decision of the Registrar of Companies to extend the filing deadline for companies with an Annual Return Date falling on 30 September 2020 or later.

Any company with an Annual Return Date of 30 September 2020 or later will be deemed to have filed on time if all elements of the annual return are completed and filed by 26 February 2021.

Further details can be found on the CRO website - https://www.cro.ie/en-ie/About-CRO/Latest-News/cro-update-regarding-filing-of-annual-returns
