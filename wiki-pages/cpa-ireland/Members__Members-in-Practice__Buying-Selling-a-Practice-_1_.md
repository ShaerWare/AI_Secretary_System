# Practice Link

Due to the amalgamation, Chartered Accountants Ireland and CPA Ireland elected to join together as a single professional body. Ultimately all of CPA’s members, students, operations, and services will be incorporated into those of Chartered Accountants Ireland.

Practice Connect has now been replaced by Practice Link. Practice Link is a new service for members wishing to advertise selling a practice/block of fees or express an interest in buying a practice/block of fees. Please select the link below to find out more information:
