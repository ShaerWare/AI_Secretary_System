# CPA Ireland release 2018 examination results statistics

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland have issued the 2018 examination results statistics, the strong results illustrate the calibre of students currently succeeding and progressing through each stage of the qualification.

Commenting on the results CPA Ireland President Cormac Mohan has said “I would like to congratulate all our CPA Ireland students on their commitment and efforts in preparing for and succeeding in their exams. These competitive results further illustrate CPA Ireland’s high calibre of dedicated students and the excellent education services that our approved educators provide”.
