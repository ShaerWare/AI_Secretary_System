# CPA Ireland Sponsorship Award with South East Technological University Carlow

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

CPA Ireland Sponsorship award for the winning team from the Strategic Analysis Module.

CPA Ireland was delighted to sponsor the award for the winning team from the Strategic Analysis Module on the Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Accounting Year 3 in South East Technological University Carlow.

Congratulations to John McDermott, Dylan Heavey, and Tristan Groenewald for your excellent work and great achievement.

Pictured left to right:
Tracy Byrne (Lecturer & Programme Director, BA (Hons) in Accounting), Adam O'Reilly (CPA Ireland), Tristan Groenewald (Prize Winner), Dylan Heavey (Prize Winner), John McDermott (Prize Winner) & Dr. Ed Dennehy – (Lecturer in Business Management)
