# IAASA publishes its 2017 Profile of the Profession

> Source: https://www.cpaireland.ie
> Site: CPA Ireland
> Scraped: 2026-04-16

IAASA has published its annual Profile of the Profession, which contains statistical data regarding the nine Prescribed Accountancy Bodies (‘PABs’) within IAASA’s supervisory remit in Ireland for the year 2017.

The Profile, which was compiled with the assistance of the PABs, presents an overview of the PABs’ memberships, student numbers and profiles, as well as the nature and scale of their regulatory and monitoring activities.
