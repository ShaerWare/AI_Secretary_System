### Post by POBox19 (2020-07-22T12:39:13+00:00)

The Motor Travel Rates were updated in Circular 16-22 issued September 2022. As you will be charging the business with the use of your personal vehicle you will not be able to recoup any vat on the running costs. You should keep a record of mileage for each expense claim. If you will be using a lot of toll roads use a toll tag account to pay the charges. This could save you a lot of hassle down the line if ever you get a revenue audit.Link to the file download: https://www.gov.ie/en/circular/378ca-ciruclar-16-2022-motor-travel-rates/
