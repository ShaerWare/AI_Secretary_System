### Post by TheDigitalAccountant (2025-05-14T14:16:24+00:00)

Depends on the business. If you purchase diesel from a petrol station or service station they will issue you a receipt which will have all the information included to be a detailed receipt. If you purchase from a company or business that provides a service they are like to issue you with an invoice or bill. And once that invoice is paid they will then issue you with a receipt. It completely depends on the context and what you are purchasing.
