### Post by ari101 (2009-07-02T12:44:23+00:00)

You charge VAT on all sales not just those over 37.5k when registered.If you expect to exceed the threshold in 12 months from now you would be expected to register now, regardless of it crossing over two financial years.If you are dealing with an Irish VAT registered business, quoting for the job stating the fee quoted is exclusive of VAT might be one way to cover this off.You should also review your existing clientele to determine who might be impacted by registering (e.g. consumers and non VAT reg entities who can't claim the VAT back), these contracts might take a hit /require renegotiation.
