### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

You're actually expected to register from when your anticipated sales reach the thresholds in any continuous 12 month period, €85,000 for sales at present and €42,500 for services, rather than when you actually hit that turnover level. For example, if you purchase €40,000 worth of goods for resale between Jan and March 2026 in a business that normally has a 4 times markup, you'd be anticipating sales of €160,000 in the coming months hence the requirement kicks in. Suppliers from outside Ireland should zero rate their goods to you as a B2B supply of goods. You would then be required to account for the VAT on your VAT returns, there are different rules whether its EU or non-EU. I'd recommend engaging an accountant because it's not straight forward and quite a bit to get your head around, your time will likely be better spent building the business than worrying about the VAT
