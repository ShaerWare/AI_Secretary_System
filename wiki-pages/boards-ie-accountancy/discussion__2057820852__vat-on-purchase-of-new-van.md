### Post by calii (2025-02-23T21:57:16+00:00)

If you’re VAT registered and purchasing a new van for your business, you can reclaim the VAT on the purchase through your VAT returns. The VAT is typically reclaimed in full in the VAT return period when the van is purchased, reducing your overall VAT liability. Keep all invoices and ensure the van is used for business purposes to qualify. For accurate calculations, use tools like CalcVAT to determine reclaimable amounts. Consult your accountant to ensure compliance with VAT rules and proper documentation.



### Post by CWMMC (2021-11-13T11:46:12+00:00)

https://www.boards.ie/discussion/2057820852/vat-on-purchase-of-new-van

In regards to the purchase of a van, you would get the VAT back in the period that it is due, so for example if you buy the van today, you would be claiming the VAT back in March in your January/February return. . (depends if your bi-monthly but just an example). You would need to keep the receipt and invoice and pass that into your accountant. The invoice needs all the details on it as well as all the information to make sure that it is a VAT invoice. Depending on your Van there might be VAT charged on selling it which will need to be accounted into your VAT return as well.
