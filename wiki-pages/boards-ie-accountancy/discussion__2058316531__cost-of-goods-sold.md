### Post by DUBLINIRL (2020-07-15T10:54:46+00:00)

It's not really that important which you pick so long as you are consistent year on year.It is a bit more relevant for business that have a lot of inventory as you are allowed to add the cost of shipping to the price you paid the supplier for the goods to get your inventory value.The effect of this?... the cost of the shipping from the supplier to you (carriage inwards) does not hit your P&L until you sell the items to your customer.... in basic terms it remains an asset just transferring the amount of the carriage in from money in the bank to inventory.When you post something to Shipping and delivery expense (Expense) that hits the P&L immediately.
