### Post by Bobby1984 (2009-09-07T22:36:33+00:00)

Without knowing details of your business or your situation, the following is a general statement. You can only expense business mileage to the company. Driving to and home from work are not business mileage.PCP can impose penalties if you exceed a certain mileage for the year. A number of factors can affect the final value of the car and if you intend on using it as a trade in against a new PCP, it may not have the value required.Once you get in to PCP, you can tend to get sucked in and continuously keep entering in to new agreements. You will never own a car unless you make the balloon payment at the end but then you have an old(ish) car that may need more repairs which can be expensive.

That's the simplest way but the most cost effective way would be to buy it through your company. An EV has big tax benefits, at least for the moment, you could effectively buy one from company funds completely instead of paying yourself, paying tax on that and buying a personal car from already taxed income.You'll need to understand the tax laws and thresholds etc or have an accountant that does.One of my cars is through my company 100% and is completely tax free, the other is private because it's over thresholds. So it depends on what type of car you want to buy and how much you want to spend.
