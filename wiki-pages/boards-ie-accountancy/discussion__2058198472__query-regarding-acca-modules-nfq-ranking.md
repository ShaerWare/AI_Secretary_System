### Post by ari101 (2009-07-02T12:44:23+00:00)

Generally the current F1-F3 equivalent exams gain you an ACCA Diploma in Accounting & Business. They are more or less equivalent to the content of most Accounting Technician exams (these are what you get exemption from if you go for ACCA and already have a technician qualification). This Dip has UK RFQ 4 status, which is generally considered equivalent of NFQ 6, however it has not been awarded that status here.See: https://www.accaglobal.com/an/en/student/exam-entry-and-administration/exam-results/types-of-certificates.htmlBest suggestion - try see if you can get a pdf cert for the Dip. Login in to your myacca if you still have access and see if you can download a copy or contact ACCA and ask. No guarantees they accept as equivalent but it might be worth a shot.
