### Post by J.Ryan (2009-12-01T17:15:37+00:00)

All Certified Public Accountants will change to Chartered later this year, due to the merger of the two institutes, the other type is Chartered Certified,  which just means that they belong to a different Professional Association.  The main thing is to ensure that they are members of a Professional Association,  the term accountant, isn't protected by any legislation (currently) in Ireland.  They are the two biggest professional bodies in the country, but, there are others.Ask in your friend group or your work contacts for a recommendation,  personally I prefer clients that are recommended by existing clients.
