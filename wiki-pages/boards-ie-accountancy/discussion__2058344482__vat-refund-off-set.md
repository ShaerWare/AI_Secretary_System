Hi, I'm due a VAT refund let’s say €1500 end of March.  I've opted for an off set of the €1500 VAT against the current March PAYE liabilities which is €750 and the next April PAYE liabilities (which I don't know yet but took a bet that it'll be) €750. So 1500-750=750 VAT end March and 750-750=0 VAT end of April. Now I hope that everyone can see my challenge since I'm not getting a VAT refund in the bank nor I'm paying PAYE from the bank. End of March Financial account is showing:Creditor Revenue Vat €1500 ( I know how compute a vat refund but not how to off-set it against liabilities)Creditor Revenue PAYE €-750 (this usually is dealt with €750 out of bank then closing balance will be €0, but this time I'm not paying out from the bank)Any helps would be much appreciated.Thanks AM

### Post by Allinall (2016-04-06T09:29:34+00:00)

https://www.boards.ie/discussion/2058344482/vat-refund-off-set

Your VAT balance will be a debtor. You just leave the two account balances sit as you outlined. At the end of April you will have an additional PAYE liability of €750, giving a total of €1,500.You can then do a journal between the two accounts to clear both balances.You will, of course have a new VAT balance at the end of April, reflecting your March-April VAT return.
