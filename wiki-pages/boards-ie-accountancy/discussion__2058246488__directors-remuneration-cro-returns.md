Disclosures in the financial statements with regard to directors remuneration should disclose the amounts paid to directors,  directors spouses and children and specify the amount if preferred.(Section 305 CA 2014 and Section 33.2a of FRS 102)• Any transaction other than wages should also be disclosed.(Section 307-309 CA 2014 and Section 33.2a of FRS 102)
