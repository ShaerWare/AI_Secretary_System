Sage although isn't perfect would be one of the best if not the best options in Ireland. Big Red Cloud/Book depending on which you use are very very beginner and don't over much if not any options to change, whilst giving the bare minimum report. Surf is like a cheaper version of Sage and I would advise you to stay as far away from it as you can. I've had a few clients on this software and I needed to take them off it, it was nothing but a headache and problems with false promises to fix it. I know recently they have made a few improvements but it's a no from me. Depending if you are using Sage Accounts or Sage online there is a few differences between them now. I'd recommend Sage one/online as it gives you graphs and can be accessed anywhere aslong as you have the login information and it sounds like the FreeAgents that you were talking about.
