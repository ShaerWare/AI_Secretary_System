### Post by Jim2007 (2010-05-24T15:19:49+00:00)

Basically you are making a YouTube video for them to watch later like they do any other video.  And they will, probably unintentionally, react in the same way - move on after the first 10 seconds if they don't like what they see.  The reality is that the people who will past that filter are the ones that know and can practice the art of making an attention grabbing YouTube video and that means you are going to have to sell yourself in the first 10 seconds, because most of the rest of what you say and do will never get heard.I'd only consider an employer who engages in this kind of thing if I did not have any alternative and I'd be looking for another employer from day one.
