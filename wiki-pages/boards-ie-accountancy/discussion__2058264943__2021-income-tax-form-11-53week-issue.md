### Post by berettaman (2010-11-02T15:19:22+00:00)

Hi All,Apologies if this has been covered elsewhere but there is an issue with ROS 2021 Form 11 offline and online.Even if you tick for a 53 week payroll year it does not make any difference to the calculation of Tax using ROS offline/online.We have notified the Revenue some time ago about this anomaly and again today.While the difference may be slight in some cases it can be a considerable overpayment of tax in others.Revenue work around at the moment is to ask practitioners to manually enter the correct tax on the assessment screen and state that you don't agree with Revenue assessment. Time consuming.Thanks,Berettaman
