### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

https://www.boards.ie/discussion/2058315042/tax-ireland-uk

You do not pay 52% tax on a second jobYou will only start paying 52% tax (40% income tax, 8% USC and 4% PRSI) when you wages for the year will be above €70,000 no matter if it's 1, 2, 3 or 4 jobs40% income tax kicks once your wages will be €40,000 for the year, and the 8% rate of USC kicks in around €70,000A second job can be awkward with adjusting your tax credits and cutoffs, especially if you have irregular hours. If you have no credits or cutoff assigned to a job then you will get hit for 52% tax, however you can adjust your tax credits and cutoff real time in 2023 to help fix this. If you don't or worked a second job previously and did not do this, then once the year is over you can submit your tax return for the year to get a refund of any tax overpaid for a year. You can still submit returns for 2019-2022, if you ever did have a second job i would encourage you to do so.
