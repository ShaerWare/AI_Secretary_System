### Post by witchgirl26 (2009-12-18T10:34:38+00:00)

Hey, Yep they do fall under the small benefit threshold. So the small benefit only states that it cannot be cash. In this it gives some examples and hampers and easter eggs are actually included in them. They might not want to go higher on the voucher in case there is another qualifying small benefit that arises as the maximum number of these is 5 with a cumulative of €1,500. https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/tdm/income-tax-capital-gains-tax-corporation-tax/part-05/05-01-01e.pdf
