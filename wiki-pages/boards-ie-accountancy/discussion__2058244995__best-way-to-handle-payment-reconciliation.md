### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

In that case, you would give the bookkeeper your bank statements, they'd be able to process the full bank. Usually the primary source of data in the accounts will be the bank statements. For the €500 that comes from Paypal/Stripe, you could give them a summary from Paypal and Stripe, there would be transactions summaries available on both of these. If all transactions were the same nature, they could probably just have the €500 from Paypal that is one entry into your bank account go straight into sales as one entry. The summary of the 500 individual transactions would just be the backup that proves the €500.If the lodgements were of a different nature they could analysed into their different places. It could work the same as if you spent €600 in Currys, €500 on a laptop and €100 on ink would be posted to 2 different places in your accounts.
