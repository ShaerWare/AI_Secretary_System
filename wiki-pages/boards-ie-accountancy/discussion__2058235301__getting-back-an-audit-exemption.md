### Post by capefear (2011-05-01T20:25:00+00:00)

Once you have submitted the return that was late you can’t get the audit exemption back. You have to apply for the courts to get the exemption back on the return that’s late. If the court allows you to get your audit exemption back you are giving 28 days to file the return that is late and you are not charged late fees and you carry on as normal.
