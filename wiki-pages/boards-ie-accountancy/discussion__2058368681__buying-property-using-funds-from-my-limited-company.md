### Post by Bobby1984 (2009-09-07T22:36:33+00:00)

Specific structures need to be in place when lending from one company to another. It seems to be an unnecessary step to set up a new company if everything is on in your current one. Have you thought about your current company contributing to your pension and buying it through that (in your own name and not in the company name)?
