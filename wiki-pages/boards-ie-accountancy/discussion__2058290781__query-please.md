### Post by CWMMC (2021-11-13T11:46:12+00:00)

There is many different ways of doing it but the CRO will not release that information. They are just a Registration office in which they just file the returns. I believe if you go onto solocheck.ie or search4less they can give you the accounts information's. I believe with solocheck it costs less than €5 for the report and then with search4less its a monthly subscription for accounting practices.



### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

You will get limited information from most LTD companies from the CRO as the majority file abridged accounts with limited information. You will be able to see the profit for the year, but it's hard to deduce much from profit alone. As above the likes of solocheck will give you the accounts for a small feeVAT details are not public information, even for larger companies. The abridged accounts are the only financial information available to the public
