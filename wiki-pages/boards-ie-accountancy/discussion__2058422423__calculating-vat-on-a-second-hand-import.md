### Post by ELM327 (2017-02-24T19:42:09+00:00)

https://www.boards.ie/discussion/2058422423/calculating-vat-on-a-second-hand-import

Vat is paid on the actual amount you paid - which includes the cost price of the vehicle (handwritten receipt will suffice once the price paid is realistic), and also the cost of getting it here, either ferry ticket or shipping waybill/bill of lading will suffice here.So if you paid 5k for a 2010 Audi A6 for instance, and spent 800 on the ferry you'd have (5800*1.23) as the vat and then (5800*1.23*1.10) as the total after vat and customs.You'd then have to pay VRT which is based on emissions and also on revenue's OMSP not on what you paid.
