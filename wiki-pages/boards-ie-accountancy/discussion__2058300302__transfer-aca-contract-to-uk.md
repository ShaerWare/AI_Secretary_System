### Post by Jim2007 (2010-05-24T15:19:49+00:00)

To the best of my knowledge there are only reciprocal rights for members not students.  That is not to say one of the British institutes would not be willing to take your situation into account it’s just at their discretion not a right.  Get in contact with them and see what they say.  I’d expect they’d want your current employer to sign off on your training record at a minimum.
