That's called fraud and revenue are very very good at finding fraud.You can reclaim vat if you make a purchase in relation to a vat registered business you operate. The vat is paid to the seller. You then make a vat return to the revenue showing you vatable income and vatable expense.If there is a difference you either pay revenue or if your vatable expenses are higher than your vatable income, you request a refund.If you show no vatable income and only a vatable expense, this is a red flag and you would likely be subject to a scoping enquiry which could lead to a full audit.

### Post by witchgirl26 (2009-12-18T10:34:38+00:00)

OP it's also not just as straight forward as "setting up a company". You'd have to file accounts (even abridged ones) with companies office annually & there are fines if their missed. You can be subjected to an audit at any point at which you have to produce the last 3 years. If you're continuously filing accounts with nothing going through them except the car, it's going to raise flags. You also have to an annual return to revenue which they'd spot something on. There's a reason it's not done by people - lots of obstacles for doing it for the wrong reasons & not much benefit in the long run.
