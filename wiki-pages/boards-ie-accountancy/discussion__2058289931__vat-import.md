Hello Marh,Yes, you can avail of Postponed Accounting (without the need to engage with an accountant - who very often would not know the finer points of how Customs & Excise works in relation to VAT on Imports).Postponed accounting is one of the few - if not only - benefits of Brexit. If you are registered for VAT/EORI you can ask the customs broker to apply for Postponed Accounting on the customs entry. If you are in good standing with Revenue that request will be granted. It means that instead of paying VAT on arrival and then claim it back through your periodic VAT return, you account for the import transaction in your VAT return. That avoids cash outlay and makes it a purely administrative transaction.Feel free to DM me if you require any further assistanceCheers,Rudolf289
