### Post by conor1979 (2011-07-19T16:18:34+00:00)

Your boyfriend does not have to pay tax on your inheritance, it is not being given to him and has nothing to do with him. Make sure that when your Dad gives you the money that it is well documented that it is exactly that, your inheritance. Have it paid in to your own personal bank account, not a joint account.What you do with it after that is up to you. If you go to get a mortgage the bank will ask you where you got the money from i.e savings, inheritance, etc so having the details of it all documented will help, they may also ask for a letter from your Dad stating it was your inheritance and he has no rights to the house.
