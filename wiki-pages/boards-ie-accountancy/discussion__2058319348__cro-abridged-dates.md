### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

First of all you should only have one financial year end, and use that for both CRO and Revenue. Have you submitted any CT returns to Revenue? You should ensure to use the same financial year end as the Revenue submission.I would urge you to contact an accountant to prepare the accounts and B1 because you will find yourself in bother with the CRO and Revenue trying to do it yourself. While the ARD is 20/11/23 the deadline to file is 56 days after this, so while you might not have much success with accountants in the next few weeks in the lead up to the income tax filing deadline of 15/11/23 you still have plenty of time to get things right.
