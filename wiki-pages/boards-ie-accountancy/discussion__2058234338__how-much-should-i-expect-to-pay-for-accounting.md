### Post by Jim2007 (2010-05-24T15:19:49+00:00)

It will depend very much on what you want them to do....Some people rock up at the end of the year with a shoebox full of documents and expect the accountant to produce everything up to and including final accounts and tax submissions.  In such a case someone has to sit down and go through it, querying the client as they go along.Some will come with the basic bookkeeping done and the accountant extracts the final accounts and does the tax submissionA few come with even the final accounts done and just want a reviewWhere are you?
