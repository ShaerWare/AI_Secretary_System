### Post by SureYWouldntYa (2011-10-22T23:25:42+00:00)

It depends on the loan agreement and who will get the hit/benefit of the exchange difference. If the agreement is to pay back €50k, this would cost the Irish company £45k and they have the £1k/€1.25k loss so it is reflected in their accounts. The UK company loaned £50k, it got back £50k so no gain no loss for them. However, if the agreement is to pay back €50k then you have the opposite where the UK company gets the hit/benefitJournal is correct and will be the same no matter which company it is
