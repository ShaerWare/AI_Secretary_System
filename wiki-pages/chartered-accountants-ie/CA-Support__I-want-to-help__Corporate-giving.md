# Corporate giving

Can your company help support the most vulnerable members of the accountancy profession? Every business has accountants, and like everyone else, they can fall on hard times. Bereavement, depression, illness and family crisis can happen to us when we least expect it. With your generosity, CA Support can help chartered accountants, accounting technicians, students and family members through these adversities.

It is always worth remembering; a happy and healthy member of staff is a productive member of staff.

Whether you are a small or large business, we can tailor a partnership that is mutually beneficial and that will accommodate you and your staff. From once-off fundraising initiatives to a year-long or multi-year charity partnership, you can choose a level of involvement that’s the right fit for your business.

#### A partnership with CA Support can help you to:

- Provide fun and motivational ways to engage your staff
- Raise the profile of your business
- Support members of your staff who are going through adversity

#### Charity of the Year

By choosing CA Support as your company’s Charity of the Year, you can build a strong personal relationship with a charity that promises to care for members of the accountancy community who are suffering hardship. CA Support will work to develop a programme of activities that fits with your company’s Corporate Social Responsibility objectives.

#### Corporate Giving

Corporate gifts are a crucial source to us. All corporate donations to CA Support are eligible for tax relief at the corporate rate, reducing the actual cost of the donation.

#### Company Fundraising Events

Having staff members get together to fundraise in aid of charity can foster great workplace camaraderie. Why not organise bake sales, coffee mornings, quizzes, raffles or ideas of your own in aid of CA Support? If you are interested in doing a corporate fundraiser, please contact our CA Support Fundraising and Marketing Executive. We will support you throughout your event. We can provide posters, collection buckets, money boxes and t-shirts.

#### Payroll giving

Payroll-giving is a simple and effective way for employees to make regular payments to a charity directly from their salary. Encouraging your employees to give a regular gift to CA Support in this manner is a great way of helping the most vulnerable members of the accountancy community.

#### Matched giving

Matched Giving is when an organisation matches the amount of fundraising an employee does for a charity. The fundraising of the employee, or group of employees, is matched by the company's own resource up to a pre-determined level.

A matched funding programme is a great way of encouraging employees to fundraise for CA Support as they’ll feel their fundraising will have a bigger impact.

#### How we can work together

CA Support would be delighted to develop a relationship with your company and to tailor a fundraising programme which will be beneficial to us both. We will ensure our tailor-made programme will meet your objectives and can be integrated into your plans.
