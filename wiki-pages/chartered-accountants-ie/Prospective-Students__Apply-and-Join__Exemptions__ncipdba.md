# National College of Ireland NCI

## Exemption criteria - Postgraduate Diploma in Business in Accounting / MSc in Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1) candidates must achieve the award of Postgraduate Diploma in Business in Accounting and/or Master of Science in Accounting; and a mark of not less than 50% in key modules as set out below. Further details of criteria are available from the university directly.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Advanced Financial Management (minimum 50%)             Financial Management (minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | Performance Measurement (minimum 50%)             Management Accounting (minimum 50%) |
| Financial Accounting | Financial Reporting (minimum 50%)             Financial Accounting (minimum 50%) |
| Taxation | Advanced Taxation (minimum 50%)             Taxation (minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | Company Law (minimum 50%) |
