# Corporate A-Z: JDM Insurance Services Ltd

## About JDM Insurance Services Ltd – Providing Insurance Solutions for Chartered Accountant Ireland Members

JDM Insurance Services Ltd offers specialist insurance solutions for accountants, including but not limited to Professional Indemnity Insurance, Office Insurance, Cyber Insurance, and Directors & Officers Insurance.

JDM is an independent insurance broker with more than 30 years’ experience supporting the insurance needs of accountants. We place strong importance on building long‑term, trusted relationships with every client.

We are a well‑established firm, highly respected within the insurance market, and relied upon by accountancy practices nationwide. Our skilled and experienced team provides a personalised service, ensuring each client receives advice and cover tailored to their specific requirements. At JDM, our clients always come first.

## Why choose JDM Insurance Services Ltd?

- We are one of the leading insurance brokers specialising in cover for accountants.
- We treat our clients the way we would expect to be treated - professionally, transparently, and with genuine care.
- Our team understands the unique risks faced by accountancy practices and provides expert guidance on the insurance products designed to address them.
- If a claim arises, we support clients through every step of the process, working to ensure an efficient resolution and the best possible outcome.
- We recognise that every client is different and work to secure policies that reflect each firm’s specific needs.
- Through our Professional Indemnity scheme with Zurich, we can arrange competitively priced cover for accountants.
- Our goal is to give you peace of mind so you can focus on growing your business, knowing we’re only a phone call away should you need assistance.
