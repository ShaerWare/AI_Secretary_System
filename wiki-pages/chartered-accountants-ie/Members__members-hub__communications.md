# Communications

Are you receiving the most relevant communications from your Institute?

We offer a wide range of newsletters and bulletins that allow you to hear about topics that matter to you - either in a digest or in detail from one of our specialised teams.

Log in here and visit the MyAccount area to review your communications preferences, including newsletters and topics of interest, so we can keep you fully informed.

This video format is not supported

In addition to the newsletters and bulletins listed below, members can indicate relevant industry sectors and topics of interest in the preference section to receive curated updates and notifications related to their interests.

- Briefly - weekly, published by Accountancy Ireland
- District Society newsletters - choose to hear from Australia, Cork, Leinster, London, Mid-West, North West, Ulster, US, Western
- Business Network Groups
- Professional Development Weekly - weekly update on CPD courses, conferences and qualifications
- eNews - weekly digest of Chartered Accountants News
- Member Benefits - quarterly newsletter for partner offers and discounts
- Overseas Members
- Practice Consulting - updates for members in practice from Practice Consulting team
- Publishing
- Tax News - weekly bulletin from Tax team with sections focussed on developments in Ireland, UK and Europe
- Thrive
- ACA Professionals

We also run a Student newsletter, updates from CASSI student society, and Accountancy Ireland Extra for trainees and third-level students.
