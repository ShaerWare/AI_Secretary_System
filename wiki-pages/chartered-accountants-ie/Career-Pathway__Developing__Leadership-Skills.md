Leadership skills

## What do they look like?

You may have headed some projects and gained experience with activities outside your working life, all of which add to your judgement.

Your leadership skills are your ability to influence those around you - to inspire, guide, and support

### Team leadership - What are the landmarks?

- Provide clear direction and priorities to a team
- Delegate authority to match responsibility and hold staff accountable for successful execution
- Organise teamwork to encourage co-operation and bring together complementary skills and expertise

### Agile thinking - What are the landmarks?

- Identify and consider emerging development opportunities and risks when articulating new options and recommendations
- Maintain a broad, strategic perspective while identifying and focussing on crucial details

### Diplomatic sensitivity/emotional intelligence - What are the landmarks?

- Be aware of the impact your behaviours are having on others and be able to read and adjust to the behaviours of others appropriately

### Organisational alignment - What are the landmarks?

- Promote a shared understanding of the organisation's needs and strategic direction to motivate your teams

### Strategic networking - What are the landmarks?

- Identify and create opportunities to initiate new partnerships that will facilitate the achievement of goals

### Mentoring and coaching - What are the landmarks?

- Be able to mentor and coach others with a view to developing them

## Development activities

#### How can you get there?

Here are our suggestions for boosting your skills in the three main areas at this stage of your career.
Don't forget, many of these are FREE!

- Continue to refer to this Career Pathway template to set goals, improve performance and identify gaps
- Continue to use the free Chartered Accountants Ireland career coaching services
- Join a Chartered Accountants Ireland Committee - like the Young Professionals District Society
- Become involved in your District society
- Attend Chartered Accountants Ireland society and committee events
- Do some more CPD training in soft skills/people management
- Join Toastmasters
- Join a representative industry group
- Coach or involve yourself with sports teams
- Watch relevant YouTube Tedtalks
- Read articles in the Harvard Business Review
- Get board experience - volunteer on a board
- Complete psychometric testing to better understand your personality traits and skills
- Seek 360 degree feedback in the workplace
- Create and manage your professional brand
- Have a professional presence on LinkedIn
- Learn about emotional intelligence and develop yours
