# Maynooth University

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business Studies in Business & Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and the specific grade requirements in key modules as set out below. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FN206 Financial Management (Minimum 50%)             FN205 Corporate Finance (Minimum 40%)             AC160 Fundamentals of Finance and Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Management Accounting | AC305 Advanced Management and Cost Accounting (Minimum 50%)             AC205 Management and Cost Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Financial Accounting | AC313 Advanced Financial Reporting (Minimum 50%)             AC202 Financial Accounting 3 (Minimum 40%)              AC201 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum 40%)             AC152 Financial Accounting 1 (Minimum 40%)             AC151 Introduction to Accounting  (Minimum 40%) |
| Taxation | AC207 Personal and Business Taxation (Minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | LW480 Law & Governance  (Minimum 50%)             LW180 Business Law & Legal Process (Minimum 40%) |
