# Independent College

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Arts (Hons) in Accounting and Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree and a minimum mark of 50% in all modules listed below. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Corporate Finance             Financial Risk Management |
| Management Accounting | Management Accounting             Advanced Management Accounting              Performance Management              Advanced Performance Management |
| Financial Accounting | Introduction to Financial Accounting              Financial Accounting             Advanced Financial Accounting             Advanced Financial Accounting: Implementation Issues             International Financial Reporting: Frameworks and Standards |
| Taxation | Income Tax             Corporate Tax             Indirect Taxes             Capital Gains Taxes & Capital Acquisitions Taxes |
| Law for Accountants | Legal Issues in Business             Legal Issues in Corporate Entities, Management and Regulation              Legal Issues in Corporate Financing and Associated Processes |
