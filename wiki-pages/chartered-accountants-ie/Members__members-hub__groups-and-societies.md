# Groups and societies

Get more from your membership by engaging with committees, District Societies and network groups that are relevant to you.

Committees focus on a particular professional topic and help shape the work of the Institute. District Societies and international chapters connect Institute members in a specific area or city, offering events that range from topical talks to social gatherings. Network Groups bring together members with shared interests and goals.

Follow the links to find out more about how to get involved with societies and groups through the Institute.
