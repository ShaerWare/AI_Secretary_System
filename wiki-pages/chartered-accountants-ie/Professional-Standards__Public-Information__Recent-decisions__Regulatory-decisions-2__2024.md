# Regulatory decisions 2024

## Regulatory decisions 2024

- Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate














    (?) 04/03/2025 14:53:56














    (?) The Institute of Chartered Accountants in Ireland regulates its members in accordance with the provisions of its Bye Laws openly and in the public interest. Oversight of this role is performed independently by the Professional Standards Board. The Institute hereby places notice that:
Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate
 The Quality Assurance Committee made an order to withdraw the practising certificate of a member for failing to comply with the requirements of the Public Practice Regulations. This order took effect on 10 October 2024. On this occasion, the Committee exercised its discretion not to include the name of the member and firm in the publication. Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate
- Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate and firm’s audit registration and DPB licence














    (?) 04/03/2025 14:53:54














    (?) The Institute of Chartered Accountants in Ireland regulates its members in accordance with the provisions of its Bye Laws openly and in the public interest. Oversight of this role is performed by the Professional Standards Board. The Institute hereby places notice that:
Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate and firm’s audit registration and DPB licence
 The Quality Assurance Committee made an order to withdraw the practising certificate of William Barry Thompson for failing to comply with the requirements of the Public Practice Regulations. The Quality Assurance Committee made an order to withdraw the audit registration and Designated Professional Body (“DPB”) licence of the firm Barry Thompson and Company, of 76 Church Street, Portadown, Craigavon, Co. Armagh, BT62 3EU for failing to comply with the requirements of the Audit Regulations and DPB Regulations. This order took effect on 1 October 2024. Withdrawal of a member’s practising certificate and firm’s audit registration and DPB licence
