# Corporate A-Z: Circle K

## Circle K sustainability journey.

We have a mission at Circle K to make our customers’ lives a little easier every day. We are committed to making a positive impact and being part of the solutions in the communities where we work and live. Sustainability is an integral part of our business, and creating a more responsible future is increasingly important to who we are as a company.

As part of this sustainability strategy, we are rolling out a HVO and EV charging capability on our network.  Click here to learn more about these initiatives ranging from solar energy and renewable fuels to our diversity and inclusion initiatives.

We would like to provide you with more information about HVO, which may be of interest in relation to obligations arising from Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive.

Take a look at the HVO Leaflet and please contact us on HVO@circlekeurope.com to discuss availability & pricing.

## Lower your company transportation costs by joining Circle K Commercial Fleet fuel card scheme.

As a member you can lower your company transportation costs by joining Circle K commercial fleet fuel card scheme. With over 450 service stations, Circle K has the largest branded fuel card network in Ireland.

This gives you great choice and opportunity to service your business needs anywhere in Ireland.
