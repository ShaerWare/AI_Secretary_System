# Retirement

## Benefits for retired members

- Non-executive directorship recruitment service
- Access to part time or voluntary roles
- Access to free CPD and events
- Invitation to get involved in the Institute’s Mentor Programme
- Opportunity to get involved in committees
- District Society events and networking (golf and social activities)
- Golden Jubilee

Did you know?  Starting January 1st, following your 40th year of membership, your annual subscription will be reduced to 0.00. This discount will apply automatically every year thereafter.

We truly value your continued association with Chartered Accountants Ireland. If you have any questions or would like to discuss this further, please reach out to our team using the Query Form below, and we’ll be happy to assist you.
