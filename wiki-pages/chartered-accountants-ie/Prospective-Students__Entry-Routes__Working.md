## Training pathway: Flexible Route

Our Flexible Route pathway involves the same education, exams and work experience requirements and ultimately ends with gaining the same ACA qualification. But with the Flexible Route, the education and experience elements do not have to run concurrently.

This pathway allows students to study on their own terms, students don't have to be working in a relevant role when they begin their studies, they can start their exams first and generate the relevant work experience further down the line.

- Flexibility: Stay in your current role and record the relevant accountancy experience now or further down the line.
- Study on your own terms: You choose how many exams you take. Up to 8 years to qualify.
- Qualify faster: our exam success rate is higher than other accountancy bodies
- Fewer exams: when compared with other accountancy bodies
- The best potential for career progression in business
