# Partnerships

Get more from your membership via the Institute's partnerships in the wider business and finance community.

Your membership gives you access to offers and discounts from a range of partner organisations - find out more in the Member Benefits area. You must be logged in to access these members-only pages.

Members of the Institute are also part of an international network of groups that are shaping the future of finance. This includes accountancy bodies like IFAC or Chartered Accountants Worldwide, who organise global events like One Young World with help from engaged members here in Ireland.
