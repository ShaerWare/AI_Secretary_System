# Trinity College Dublin TCD

## Exemption criteria - Business, Economics and Social Studies

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree and have a clear pass, without compensation, in all the subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in key modules indicated below. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | BUU 22550 Introduction to Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | BUU 33522 Management Accounting for Business Decisions II             BUU 33521 Management Accounting for Business Decisions I             (Average mark of 50% with not less than 40% in either paper required) |
| Financial Accounting | BUU 44532 Financial Reporting & Analysis II             BUU 44531 Financial Reporting & Analysis I             (Average mark of 50% with not less than 40% in either paper required)                          BUU 33532 Financial Accounting II (Minimum mark of 40% required)             BUU 33531 Financial Accounting I (Minimum mark of 40% required)             BUU 22530 Introduction to Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | BUU 33730 Taxation II             BUU 33720 Taxation I              (Average mark of 50% with not less than 40% in either paper required) |
| Law for Accountants | BUU 44660 Company & Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
