# Consultations

The Board engages in consultations in respect of significant decisions which may affect key stakeholders. The Board both initiates consultations and participates in external consultations.

## Professional Standards Board consultations

The Board will engage with key stakeholders on issues relating to its regulatory responsibilities, for example proposals to amend the various rules and regulations for which it is responsible.

## External consultations

The Board will also keep stakeholders informed of consultations being undertaken, on important regulatory issues, by other professional bodies, standard setters and oversight bodies, nationally and internationally.

Consultation papers and responses are posted to this section of the website under the relevant tabs.
