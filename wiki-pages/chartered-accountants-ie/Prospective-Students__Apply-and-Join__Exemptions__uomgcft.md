# Ulster University Derry-Londonderry

## Exemption criteria - Graduate Certificate in Finance and Taxation (Bridging programme - Derry-Londonderry)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve an overall pass or better (i.e. the award of the Graduate Certificate) and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in the relevant subjects as set out below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACF526 Finance (minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | Not awarded |
| Financial Accounting | Not awarded |
| Taxation | ACF555 Taxation (minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | Not awarded |
