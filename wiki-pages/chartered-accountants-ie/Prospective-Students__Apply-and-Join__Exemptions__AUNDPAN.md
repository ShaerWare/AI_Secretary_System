# Technological University Dublin (DIT)

## Exemption criteria - Post-Graduate Diploma in Accounting / Master of Business Studies in Accounting (International)

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall pass or better (i.e. the award of the Postgraduate Diploma); and a mark of not less than 50%, without compensation, in each module as set out below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCT9023 Financial Management |
| Management Accounting | ACCT9040 Management Accounting |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT9041 Financial Accounting |
| Taxation | TAXN9000 Taxation |
| Law for Accountants | LAW9001 Business Law |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve the award of Postgraduate Diploma (TU304) / Master (TU324); and a mark of not less than 50%, without compensation in the key module as set out below.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing & Assurance | ACCT9015 Auditing |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | N/A |
| Financial Reporting | N/A |
| Taxation II | N/A |
