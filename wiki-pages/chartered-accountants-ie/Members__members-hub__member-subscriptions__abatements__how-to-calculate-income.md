# How to calculate income

Eligibility for an abatement is based on your income. The threshold for members who wish to apply is an annual gross income of €44,425/£31,340.

Your total annual income is your estimated income for the year, including:

- Salary
- Government benefits or allowances
- Pension
- Dividends
- Income from rental property
- Interest
- Redundancy remuneration
