# Professional development

The Institute's Professional Development department offers classes, courses and conferences designed to meet the needs of Chartered Accountants in every role and every business setting - including complimentary events that are included as part of your membership.

With offerings ranging from webinars and one-day courses to specialised diploma programmes, you're sure to find your fit among our training options. We also run conferences each year that focus on developments in particular topics, plus our end-of-year CPD Blitz series which often sells out.

Institute members can avail of over 30 CPD webinars at no extra charge. You can find our library of recorded content here, which also includes complimentary careers and wellbeing content. Our Professional Standards team also offers support and guidance on CPD.
