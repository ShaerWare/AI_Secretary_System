# University College Cork UCC

## Exemption criteria - BSc Accounting (4 year degree)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC1107 Investment in Capital Assets (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC1108 Introduction to Valuation and Risk (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC2120 Corporate Financial Management (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | AC1109 Management Accounting: Principles & Concepts (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC1115 Management Accounting: Cost & Control Systems (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC4002 Management Accounting: Planning & Control (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC4006 Management Accounting: Decision Making (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | AC1103 Financial Accounting Fundamentals (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC1104 Accounting for Partnership and Companies (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC2100 International Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC2101 Consolidated Financial Statements and Ethics (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | AC2115 Introduction to Taxation (Minimum mark of 40% required)             AC4007 Taxation; Income Tax and VAT (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LW1108 Introduction to Legal System (Minimum mark of 40% required)             LW1109 Introduction to Business Law (Minimum mark of 40% required)             LW3311 Company Law (Minimum mark of 40% required) |

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below. Please note that graduates who commence their degree from 2026 onwards will require a minimum 2.i. honours degree to claim CAP2 exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing and Assurance | N/A |
| Strategic Finance and              Management Accounting | AC1115 Management Accounting: Cost and Control Systems (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC2120 Corporate Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC3014 Management Accounting: Performance Management (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC3010 Multinational Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC3022 Governance, Regulation and Control of Financial Organisations (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4002 Management Accounting: Planning and Control (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4006 Management Accounting: Decision Making             AC4009 Financial Information Analysis (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4010 International Corporate Valuation (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4119 Securities Analysis (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Financial Reporting | AC1104 Accounting for Partnerships and Companies (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC2100 International Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC2101 Consolidated Financial Statements and Ethics (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC3013 International Financial Reporting 2: Theory and Practice (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4001 Advanced Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             AC4011 Advanced Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Taxation II (ROI) | N/A |
