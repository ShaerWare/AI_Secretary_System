# In business

Chartered Accountants Ireland Members in Business serves the interests of members employed in industry, commerce and academe. Because of the differing roles and requirements of Members in Business (MiBs), special interest groups, business groups and networking events are the primary method of support, as well as Continuing Professional Development (CPD).

The Members in Business Committee oversees the development of such services and, in addition, elicits and reflects to Council the views and needs of this major membership cohort.
