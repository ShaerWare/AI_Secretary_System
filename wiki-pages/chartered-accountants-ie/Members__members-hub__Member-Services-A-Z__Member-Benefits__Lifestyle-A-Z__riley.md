# Lifestyle A-Z: Riley

## About Riley:

Riley is an Irish, female-founded period care company and we're on a mission to drive change by supplying sustainable, period care to everyone, wherever and whenever they need it. We started Riley with you in mind, because we believe you deserve to know exactly what you're putting in your body every month. That's why our products are made with 100% certified organic cotton and nothing else. They're made with zero chlorine bleach, pesticides, rayon or other harmful chemicals. Our wrappers are biodegradable and our applicators are made from sugar cane which is a renewable resource and recyclable. Better for your body and better for our planet. We also work with three charity partners to fight against period poverty by donating menstrual products to those who need them most. We'd love for you to join our community!

### Our story

We founded Riley on the belief that our bodies deserve more. Stemming from our own real-life frustrations, we knew there had to be a better way of doing period care. After some initial research, it led us down a shocking discovery path; that many mainstream period products are full of toxins and harmful chemicals. After finding all this out, we felt compelled to do something about it... And that's how Riley was born.

We are firm believers that period products are a basic human need & should be treated like toilet paper. That means readily available whenever & wherever they're needed. Which is why we are supporting corporates & facilities teams in creating an inclusive environment within the workplace while also contributing to their sustainability agendas.
