# tax.point journal – bi-monthly publication

## What is tax.point?

tax.point is the bi-monthly digital tax journal for tax professionals, prepared by Chartered Accountants Ireland. It is available for free on the Accountancy Ireland website and app.

tax.point is compiled and edited by Gearóid O'Sullivan, Tax Manager at Chartered Accountants Ireland. It is published bi-monthly and is one of the easiest ways to stay up to date with content that is both current and relevant.
