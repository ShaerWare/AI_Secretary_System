# ICD Business School

## Exemption criteria - BA Accounting and Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1(CAP1) candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Not Awarded |
| Management Accounting | Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Cost Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | Not Awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
