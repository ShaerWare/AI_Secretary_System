# Resources

On this page you will find a collection of our thought leadership publications and other resources that seek to inform, influence and have impact for benefit of the profession and the greater good, from position papers and reports to podcasts and books.

## Position papers

Extensively researched and written by experts, these position papers propose change for the good and are at the heart of the Institute’s thought leadership programme, from easing the administrative burden of businesses on the island of Ireland to supporting reforms to public sector accounting.

## Guides and reports

Leading-edge, authoritative information on developments in core, emerging and future issues affecting the profession and all sectors of the economy, as well as broader society. Well-written and concise, each guide or report helps the reader navigate through the subject at hand.

## Podcasts and videos

Podcasts and videos on a range of contemporary issues, such as artificial intelligence (AI), sustainability, diversity, equality & inclusion (DEI), the future of work and the governance of charities, each one discussed and debated by a variety of experts and commentators.

## Books

A selection of thought leadership books taking a ‘deep dive’ into topics as diverse as corporate social responsibility, charity accounting and reporting, leading with integrity, and the history of the Irish accountancy profession.
