# National College of Ireland NCI

## Exemption criteria - BA Hons Accounting & Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1) candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii hons degree. A clear pass without compensation in relevant subjects is required and candidates must secure 50% in the subjects listed below. Further details of criteria are available from the university directly.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | H7CF Corporate Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | H7MA1 Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H8MA2 Management Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Financial Accounting | H6FACC1 Financial Accounting 1  (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H7FA2 Financial Accounting 2  (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H8FR Financial Reporting  (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Taxation | H7TX1 Taxation 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H8TX2 Taxation  2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H8TX3 Taxation  3 (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | H6BLAF   Business Law for Accounting & Finance  (Minimum mark of 50% required)             H7CLAF   Company Law for Accounting & Finance  (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
