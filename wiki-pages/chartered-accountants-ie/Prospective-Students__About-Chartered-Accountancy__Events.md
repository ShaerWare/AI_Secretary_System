# Events

Throughout the summer, our virtual lunchtime sessions give attendees the opportunity to find out more about becoming a Chartered Accountant.  These webinars are open to all, whether 3rd level graduates, ATI students / graduates or career changers; anyone who is considering a professional qualification in accountancy or business.

The team will take you through the application process, the education experience and what to expect once you become ACA qualified. You will also hear from guest speakers on what the qualification has meant for their career and have the opportunity to ask your own questions about applying, fees and more. Check out our next dates below.
