# Other client services

## Financial Reporting

### Technical help with financial reporting

You can find information about company law, accounting standards, SORPs and other information at the Financial Reporting section of the Institute’s Technical Hub.

The current domestic accounting standards in issue can be found on the FRC website.

### Financial reporting Practice Toolkits

Practice Consulting have various practice toolkits available to assist you with your financial reporting needs:

- Audit Exempt Resource Pack 2022 ROI & NI Eight sets of Pro Forma financial statements
- Pro Forma Financial Statements 2020 ROI
- Pro Forma Financial Statements 2020 NI
- Financial Statements Disclosure Checklist 2020 ROI
- Audit Exempt Engagement Letter ROI

### Financial Reporting related CPD

The Practice Consulting team regularly run financial reporting themed webinars.  Look out for our emails regarding upcoming courses, and check the CPD diary.

Our team in Practice Consulting can also provide tailored in-house training, coaching and advice.

## Investment Business

Please see the Professional Standards Department’s page on Investment Business authorisation, and their investment business support and guidance.

Practice Consulting can assist you by carrying out an external Investment Business Compliance Review.

## Insolvency

Please see Professional Standards Department’s page on Insolvency authorisation, and their Insolvency support and guidance.

You can find technical guidance and helpful resources in the Insolvency section of the Institute’s Technical Hub.

## Tax

Please see The Institute’s Tax Resource Centre.

If you or a member of your staff is interested in becoming a Chartered Tax Consultant, or sitting a Diploma in Taxation, you can find out more information on the Specialist Qualifications page.

## Assistance to borrowers

Please see our information page on the Abhaile Scheme and the Mortgage Arrears Information and Advice Scheme (MAIAS).

## Have a query?

If you have a query, please refer to our Support & Services page and contact the relevant department with your query.
