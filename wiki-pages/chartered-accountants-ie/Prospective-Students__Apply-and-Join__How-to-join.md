# How to apply

## Before you begin

- You can check your exemptions online
- Understand the difference between the Training Contract Flexible Route

## Steps to follow

## 1

### Create an account

Create an account on the Chartered Accountants Ireland website. You can do so by providing some basic details about yourself on the login page.

## 2

### Submit your online application

Whether you are eligible for exemptions or not, you will need to submit your application from the exemptions and eligibility page.

## 3

### Submit your supporting documentation

- Please follow the steps given to you in the email following the submission of your online application.
- Once your documents have been received through the appropriate channel, your application will be assessed by Institute staff. As soon as your application has been processed you will receive notice of the outcome by email along with further instructions on how to enrol to the course programme.

## Ready? Start today!

## Have a query?

If you have any questions please feel free to contact us (9-5 Mon-Fri).
