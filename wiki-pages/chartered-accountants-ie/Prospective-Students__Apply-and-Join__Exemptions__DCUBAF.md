# Dublin City University DCU

## Exemption criteria - BA Accounting & Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of the criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FBA1006 Corporate Financial Management & Investments |
| Management Accounting | ACC1004 Introductory Accounting for Planning & Control             ACC1007 Management Accounting: Cost Systems             ACC1014 Management Accounting: Decision-making |
| Financial Accounting | ACC1003 Financial Accounting 1             ACC1005 International Accounting Theory & Practice             ACC1010 Advanced International Financial Accounting |
| Taxation | ACC1011 Taxation |
| Law for Accountants | LAW1002 Principles of Commercial Law in Ireland             LAW1036 Company Law for Accountants |
