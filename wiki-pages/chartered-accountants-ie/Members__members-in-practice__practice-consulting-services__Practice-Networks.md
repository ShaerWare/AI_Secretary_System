# Practice networks

We support shared learning among practitioners. We facilitate the establishment of practitioner networks throughout the country. This enables likeminded members to discuss problems, opportunities and ideas with a view to serving clients better and making firms more profitable. We provide tailored training to practice networks.

## Training tickets

Have you considered getting a training ticket for your network? It's convenient, better value for money and can be used by anyone in the company. A bargain not to be missed!

Networks are groups of chartered accountants in practice that meet regularly to discuss issues as diverse as:

- Professional standards regulation
- Emerging practice issues
- Staffing
- Technology
- Technical matters
- Charge-out rates/value pricing
- Succession planning and exit strategies

Networks of practitioners exist in various parts of the country.
If you are interested in contacting a network in your area or if you wish to set up a network in your area contact practice networks.
