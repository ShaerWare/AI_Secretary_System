# Munster Technological University (formerly CIT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) Accounting

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all relevant subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the specified modules.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FINA8008 Financial Risk Management (50%+)             FINA8007 Advanced Financial Management (40%+)             FINA7002 Financial Management Concepts (40%+)             FINA7001 Financial Management for Accountants (40%+) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT8004 Strategic Management Accounting (50%+)             ACCT7002 Advanced Management Accounting (50%+)             ACCT7001 Management Accounting (40%+)             ACCT6004 Cost & Management Accounting (40%+)             ACCT6002 Cost Accounting (40%+) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT7017 Single Entity & Group Accounting (50%+)             ACCT7014 Financial Accounting Analysis (40%+)             ACCT7015 Company & Partnership Accounting (40%+)             ACCT6009 Financial Accounting (40%+)             ACCT6007 Fundamentals of Financial Accounting (40%+) |
| Taxation | ACCT7010 Corporate Taxes, CGT, VAT (50%+)             ACCT7009 Income Tax  (40%+) |
| Law for Accountants | LEGS7011 Company Law – Capital (50%+)             LEGS7001 Company Law Fundamentals (40%+)             LEGS6002 Irish Civil Law (40%+)             LEGS6001 Irish Legal System (40%+) |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.i honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing & Assurance | N/A |
| Strategic Finance & Management Accounting | FINA8008 Financial Risk Management (50%+)             FINA8007 Advanced Financial Management (50%+)             ACCT8006 Corporate Governance (50%+)             ACCT8004 Strategic Management Accounting (50%+)             ACCT8005 Strategic Performance Eval (50%+)             ACCT7002 Advanced Management Accounting (50%+) |
| Financial Reporting | ACCT8001 Group Financial Reporting (50%+)             ACCT8002 Financial Report Single Entity (50%+)             ACCT7017 Single Entity & Group Accounting (50%+)             ACCT7014 Financial Accounting Analysis (50%+)             ACCT7015 Company & Partnership Accounting (50%+) |
| Taxation II ROI | N/A |
