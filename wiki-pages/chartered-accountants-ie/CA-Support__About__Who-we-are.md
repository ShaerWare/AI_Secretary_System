# Who we are

We’re here to support and offer a short-term financial helping hand, if you are experiencing financial trouble. If you’re a past or present Chartered Accountants Ireland or Accounting Technicians Ireland member we’re here to help.

We’re here for you, for life.  Ask us for help, that’s what we’re here for.

## Contact us

For advice and information contact Dee France, CA Support Manager.

All conversations are carried out in the strictest confidence. You can speak to us anonymously. And we don’t share your information with anyone.
