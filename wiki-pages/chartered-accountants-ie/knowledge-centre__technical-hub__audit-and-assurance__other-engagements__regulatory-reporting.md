# Regulatory reporting

The documents listed below have been prepared in consultations with a number of regulatory bodies to provide guidance for accountants and auditors when dealing with  specific issues and regulatory reporting matters.
These documents can be accessed in the Institute Technical Content section.

## Central Bank of Ireland

- Technical Release 01/2023 Safeguarding reports for payment firms
- Technical Release 07/2016 (updated March 2024) Auditors reporting in accordance with Client Asset Regulations
- Technical Release 01/2018 Irish Collective Asset-management Vehicles Act 2015 - Guidance for Auditors
- Technical Release 01/2017 Guidance for Auditors Reporting in Accordance with Investor Money Regulations for Fund Service Providers June 2017
- Technical Release 01/2015 "Section 27BA of the Central Bank Act 1997 – Guidance for Auditors"
- Technical Alert 05/2016 Update on prescribed enactments for the purposes of section 27B of the Central Bank Act 1997
- M46 Reporting to the Financial Regulator under The Central Bank and Financial Services Authority of Ireland Act 2004 (Revised Jan 2008)
- Technical Alert 02/2018 Illustrative auditor reports SFCR periods beginning on or after 17.06.16
- Technical Alert 01/2016 Reporting on regulatory returns of insurers extract from Practice Note 20(I)
- Technical Alert 01/2015 Independent Accountants Report ICAV 2015
- Technical Alert 01/2017 auditor reports on SFCR 12 May 2017
- Technical Release 13/2016 Guidance for Insurance Auditors.
- Information Sheet IS 01/2008 Reports from Auditors appointed pursuant to Section 33 of the Investment Intermediaries Act 1995 on unincorporated entities
