# School resources

A third of Chartered Accountancy students decide to pursue the career while at second-level education. Volunteer members speak in local schools and this offers a fun, easy way to inspire the next generation of Chartered Accountants. Students love to hear about members diverse career paths and ask questions.

If you would like to register as a volunteer, please don't hesitate to contact us or fill in the register for a school visit form.

## Resources for members, teachers or guidance counsellors

As a member of Chartered Accountants Ireland, the Institute really appreciate you taking time to make these school visits and tell your story to students.  You will find below all the material to help you on your school visit.
