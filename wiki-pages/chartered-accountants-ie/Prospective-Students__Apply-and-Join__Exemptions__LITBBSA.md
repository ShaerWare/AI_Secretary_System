# Atlantic Technological University (formerly Letterkenny IT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Hons) Accounting (3-year programme)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, a clear pass without compensation, in all subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the key modules set out below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Business Finance              Advanced Management Accounting & Finance                           As both of the above modules are taken in the same year - Average mark of 50% across both modules is required                           Management Accounting & Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | Advanced Management Accounting & Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)              Management Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)              Management Accounting & Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | Financial Reporting 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)              Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required)              Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)              Bookkeeping (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | Capital Gains Tax & VAT (Minimum mark of 50% required)              Business Tax  (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | Company Law & Governance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Law for Business Environment (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
