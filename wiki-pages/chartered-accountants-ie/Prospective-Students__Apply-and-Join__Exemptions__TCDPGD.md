# Trinity College Dublin TCD

## Exemption criteria - Post Graduate Diploma in Accounting

To claim exemptions from CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of 50% in the relevant subjects in each of the subject areas. Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | BU7854 Financial Management (Minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | BU7853 Management Accounting (Minimum 50%) |
| Financial Accounting | BU7851 Financial Reporting (Minimum 50%) |
| Taxation | BU7855 Taxation (Minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | BU7116 Company Law (Minimum 50%) |
