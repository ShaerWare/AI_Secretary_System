# Dublin Business School DBS

## Exemption criteria -BA (Honours) in Accounting and Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the key modules listed below. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Financial Management (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Advanced Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | Management Accounting for Performance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Cost Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Principles of Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | Taxation Systems Irish Variant (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | Business & Company Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
