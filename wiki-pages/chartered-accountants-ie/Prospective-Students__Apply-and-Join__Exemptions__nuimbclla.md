# Maynooth University

## Exemption criteria -BCL (Law & Accounting)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1(CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and the specific grade requirements in key modules as set out below. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC503 Finance for Accountants (Minimum 50%)             AC160 Fundamentals of Finance and Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Management Accounting | AC305 Advanced Management and Cost Accounting (Minimum 50%)             AC205 Management and Cost Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Financial Accounting | AC306 Ethics & Corporate Governance (Minimum 50%)             AC202 Financial Accounting 3 (Minimum 40%)             AC201 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum 40%)             AC152 Financial Accounting 1 (Minimum 40%)             AC151 Introduction to Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Taxation | AC207 Personal and Business Taxation (Minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | LW263 Company Law (Minimum 50%)             LW156 Introduction to Law II (Required for graduates of 2026 onwards-Minimum 40%)              LW155 Introduction to Law (Minimum 40%)             LW152 Contract Law (Minimum 40%)             LW151 Law of Tort (Minimum 40%) |
