# Dundalk Institute of Technology

## Exemption criteria - BA Accounting and Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree.  A mark of 50% in all relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FNCE B8006 Strategic Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT B8006 Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT B8004 Cost & Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT B8007 Corporate Reporting & Accountability (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT B8005 Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT B8002 Foundation Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | FNCE H3019 Taxation 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             FNCE H3013 Taxation 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW H1001 Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required)             LAW H1003 Company Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
