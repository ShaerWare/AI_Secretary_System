# Resources for newly admitted members

#### Congratulations and well done on becoming a Chartered Accountant! Welcome to membership of Chartered Accountants Ireland, and to the family and community of over 39,000 Chartered Accountants across the island and around the globe.

Being a Chartered Accountant opens up a world of career opportunities for you, not only in Ireland, but also internationally.
As a member, you will have our support during every step of your career. We do appreciate that your first instinct may be to take a break from engaging with us after completing the exams, however you can derive genuine benefits from engaging with us from an early stage.
