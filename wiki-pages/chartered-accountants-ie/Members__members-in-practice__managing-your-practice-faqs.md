# Managing your practice FAQs

The purpose of this section is to answer some of the most common questions practitioners have in relation to managing their practices.  If you have any further queries, please do not hesitate to contact us at practicemembers@charteredaccountants.ie.

- What help can Practice Consulting provide regarding managing my practice? Practice Consulting can help you in the following areas: Back to Top
- Practice management
- Strategic leadership
- Developing your firm
- Where can I find help with recruiting staff? The Institute’s Career Coaching and Recruitment Service can help you with hiring a Chartered Accountant or a student. Back to Top
- I wish to train students. What do I need to know? Please see here about becoming a Recognised Training Organisation (RTO). Please also see frequently asked questions for employers, and who to contact regarding student recruitment and training. Prospective students in your firm should refer to the section on our website about becoming a student. Back to Top
- I have a complaint made against me. Where can I find more information? Who can I contact to help me? The Professional Standards Department have information and guidance in relation to the complaints and disciplinary process. For more information see our designated Members’ complaints advice page. Back to Top
- I wish to incorporate my practice. What do I need to know? We have separate helpsheets on incorporating your practice in the Republic of Ireland (E1) and Northern Ireland (E2). Back to Top
- Who do I contact regarding practice mergers, acquisitions, growth and exit advisory services? Please see our Helpsheets (D) on Buying, selling or merging accountancy practices. If you wish to speak to our Consultants in Practice Consulting, please contact us. Back to Top
- What supports do the Institute provide to help me market my practice? The public and businesses can use our online directory to search for an accountant or accountancy practice by name, location, authorisations and specialisms. Firms can pay an annual fee to receive top search ranking in the directory along with other benefits by signing up for a premium listing. You can download Chartered Accountants Ireland’s logo and other marketing materials for your firm’s use. Please also be aware of the Institute’s various social media channels. Back to Top
