# Develop your firm

## Practice life cycle support

Our experienced consultants can meet with you on or off site. Consultations can be tailored to suit your needs.

The Practice Consulting team can provide support and advice to members in practice at all stages of growth and development. We can advise practitioners regarding succession, admitting new partners, and buying selling and merging practices.

## Practice management advice

We give advice on workflow, effective management and delegation, staff incentive schemes, staff recruitment, implementation of time and billing systems.

#### Strategic leadership

The Practice Consulting team can help members and their management team to set strategic goals and action plans to achieve results within defined periods. This will prove to be a winning formula for chartered accountants who want to maximise the return on their financial and emotional investment in the business.

#### Multi disciplinary review

Half day discussion based review of crucial aspects of your practice, including practice structure, staffing, marketing, procedures, and compliance, with report.

## Have a query?

If you have a query, please refer to our Support & Services page and contact the relevant department with your query.
