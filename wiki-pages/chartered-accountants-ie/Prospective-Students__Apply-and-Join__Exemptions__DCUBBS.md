# Dublin City University DCU

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business Studies

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and a mark of not less than 50% in subject FBA1003 Financial Management. Candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of the criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FBA1003 Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC1001 Financial Accounting for Decision Making (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | Not Awarded |
| Financial Accounting | Not Awarded |
| Taxation | Not Awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Not Awarded |
