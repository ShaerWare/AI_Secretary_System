# Technological University Dublin (DIT)

## Exemption criteria - BSc Business & Law

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FNST4000 Financial Strategy (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT3010 Corporate Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ADMG4000 Advanced Management Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT3009 Advanced Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT3011 Advanced Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT9207 Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT9206 Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | TAXN1000 Taxation 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT3012 Taxation 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW1031 Company Law (Minimum mark of 50% required)             LAW1503 Law of Torts (Minimum mark of 40% required)             LAW1002 Contract Law (Minimum mark of 40% required)             LAW1500 Core Legal Skills (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
