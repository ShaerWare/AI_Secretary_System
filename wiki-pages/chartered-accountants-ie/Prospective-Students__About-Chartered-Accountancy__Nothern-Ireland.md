# Studying in Northern Ireland

## Northern Ireland’s best business qualification – Chartered Accountancy

Chartered Accountants Ireland is an all-island body, with over 5,000 members and over 1,100 students based in NI. There is no milkround in Northern Ireland but we are partners with some of Northern Ireland's top employers across all sectors and our education is delivered exclusively online so is accessible no matter where you're based.

## Work in business

65% of Chartered Accountants work in business - from the academic, public and voluntary sectors through to private industry and multinationals

## No.1 in Ireland

Qualification respected and recognised by employers and recruiters

## 83% pass rate

Pass rate for FAE in 2023, well above average of other accountancy bodies
