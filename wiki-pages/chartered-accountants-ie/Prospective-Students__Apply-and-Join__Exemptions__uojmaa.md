# Ulster University Belfast

## Exemption criteria - MSc in Advanced Accounting or Postgraduate Diploma in Advanced Accounting

| CAP2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing & Assurance | ACF703 Audit, Assurance & Professional Ethics (Minimum 50%) |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | ACF704 Strategic Financial Management (Minimum 50%)             ACF721 Strategic Management Accounting (Minimum 50%) |
| Financial Reporting | ACF701 International Corporate Reporting (Minimum 50%)             ACF707 International Group Reporting (Minimum 50%) |
| Taxation II | ACF706 Advanced Tax Planning (Minimum 50%)             ACF708 Advanced Taxation (Minimum 50%) |
