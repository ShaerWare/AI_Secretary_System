# University of Galway

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Commerce (Accounting)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. Candidates must meet the criteria and have a clear pass without compensation in all relevant college options in order to qualify for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AY208 Business Finance 1 |
| Management Accounting | AY321 Management Accounting II             AY207 Management Accounting I             AY120 Accounting |
| Financial Accounting | AY3100 Advanced Principles in Auditing and Reporting OR             AY3106 Advanced Principles in Financial Reporting             AY326 International Financial Reporting III             AY325 International Financial Reporting II             AY130 Financial Reporting |
| Taxation | AY319 Tax II             AY308 Tax I |
| Law for Accountants | LW423 Advanced Business Law               LW290 Business Law II              LW190 Business Law I                              Or             LW231 Company Law II             LW229 Company Law I             LW290 Business Law II             LW190 Business Law I |
