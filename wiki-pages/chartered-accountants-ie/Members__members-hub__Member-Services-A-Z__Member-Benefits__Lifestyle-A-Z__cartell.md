# Lifestyle A-Z: Cartell

Cartell.ie is Ireland’s most trusted car history check provider, helping consumers and businesses avoid costly risks when buying or selling used vehicles. Established in 2006, Cartell has built a reputation for delivering reliable, in-depth vehicle history reports powered by real-time data from official Irish, UK and European motoring authorities.

A Cartell car history check instantly reveals crucial details about a vehicle’s past - including whether it has been written off, reported stolen, imported, clocked (had its mileage tampered with), or if there’s outstanding finance owed. It also confirms NCT history, tax status, previous ownership records and more. This level of transparency gives buyers total confidence in the cars they buy, sell and trade.

Cartell is passionate about helping motorists make safer, smarter decisions.
