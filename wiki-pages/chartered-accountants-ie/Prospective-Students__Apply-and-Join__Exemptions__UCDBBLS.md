# University College Dublin UCD

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business and Law

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

* If a semester has been studied abroad we will require a letter from UCD confirming the equivalent subjects were completed.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FIN20010 Principles of Finance*             ACC20020 Management Accounting |
| Management Accounting | ACC20020 Management Accounting*             ACC30030 Cost Management |
| Financial Accounting | ACC10040 Financial Accounting 1             ACC20010 Financial Accounting 2             ACC30020 Financial Accounting 3 |
| Taxation | ACC30010 Tax in Society |
| Law for Accountants | LAW10340 Contract: Formation              LAW10360 General Introduction to the Irish Legal System              LAW20090 Company Law 1 |
