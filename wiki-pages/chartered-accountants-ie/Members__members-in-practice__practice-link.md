# Practice Link

Practice Link is a new service for members wishing to advertise selling a practice/block of fees or express an interest in buying a practice/block of fees.

Advertisements will be posted here on our website and in our Practice Matters newsletter for six months, for a fee of €200 plus VAT (£170) . The Practice Consulting team will send all relevant queries in relation to advertisements to participating firms.  All enquiries can be sent to Practicelink@charteredaccountants.ie

Practice Consulting will continue to offer a consultation service to members who are considering buying, selling, merging, retiring, or making any other major change to their practice. This is a separate service for which we may charge an hourly rate. This advice is impartial, in that we do not “act for” one side or the other in negotiations between members. Members can avail of either or both services in accordance with their circumstances.
