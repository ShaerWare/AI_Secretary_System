# Lifestyle A-Z: Radius Connect

## A trio of offers for members from Radius Connect

Radius Connect has provided three different members benefits available to members in NI (open to new customers only).

- Cloud hosted telephony
- Mobile SIM
- Broadband FTTP & SOGEA

Why choose Radius Connect Solutions?
Radius Connect has a 30-year heritage as a provider of business telecoms products and services from all the leading networks and providers.
We integrate innovative business solutions, from mobile telephony, business phone systems and connectivity, to intelligent device security, and collaboration and productivity tools.

Why choose Radius Connect?

- Experience and expertise - trusted by customers across the globe for over 30 years
- Value - unbeatable value for money thanks to our relationships with suppliers
- Service - our award-winning customer service makes us part of your team

Radius Connect have twelve sales and technical support centres around the UK and Ireland.

Businesses choose us because our experts always recommend the products that best meet the needs of our customers, solving problems with innovative, tailor-made solutions.
We can do this because we offer a huge choice of providers and brands in every category, at market-leading prices.

Our customers choose to stay with us because of our local, qualified technical support teams, who go above and beyond to keep their communication systems running smoothly. We solve 94.6% of problems in under two hours.

## How to avail of the offer

These are three separate offers, which can be used together or alone. Members just need to email salesNI@radiusconnectsolutions.com quoting Chartered Accounts Ireland to avail of the offer(s).
