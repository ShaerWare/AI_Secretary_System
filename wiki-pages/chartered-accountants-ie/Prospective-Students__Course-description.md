## Leading  educator

With our high caliber lecturing team, quality study materials, on the ground support, flexible fee options, and easy-to-follow timetables, we feel that Chartered offers you the very best in professional accountancy education in Ireland.

## Our approach to education

Our approach to education is unique, we don’t outsource the education to third party colleges. Everything is provided for you in-house – lecturers, courses, exams, and support. We won’t ask you to select an education provider. We have the best lecturers across the Island of Ireland, experts in their field, who deliver the best syllabus to the highest standards.

## Course breakdown

## CAP1

CAP1 is the first academic level of the education programme it provides the essential foundation knowledge.

## CAP2

CAP2 builds on the foundation knowledge studied at CAP1 and follows the cumulative knowledge principal.

## FAE

FAE is designed to integrate the knowledge, skills and values derived from earlier studies and the work environment.

## Education offering

The Institutes education programme is designed to address market and employer needs, anticipate future skills requirements and to deliver supports for its student body.

The education programme includes final year specialist elective subjects making the Chartered qualifications attractive for new graduates and experienced professionals.

## Fees and payments options

Fees are payable either upfront for the academic level, or using our payment plan. You only have to pay for what you are doing each year, so if you are only taking two subjects, you only have to pay for two subjects.

The fees you see below will cover everything – the full course, first exam attempt, and all books, course notes, and other study materials. We won’t charge you a registration fee, nor do we charge you to be a student – unlike other accountancy bodies, there’s no student subscription fee.

Our payment plan allows you to pay your fee in three interest-free installments. To avail of this you can opt for this plan when you enrol onto the programme.
