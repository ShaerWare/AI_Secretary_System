# Sustainability for Accountants - A Guide

This guide is an introduction to sustainability for accountants.

It describes what to do - and where to start - to operate sustainably, successfully and cost-effectively.

## About the guide

The benefits to businesses of integrating sustainability into their strategies are clear. They include cost savings and reductions, improved attraction and retention of talented people, mitigating risk and securing investment. But for many businesses, sustainability can seem unstructured or intimidating, without clear guidelines on how to achieve it.

The purpose of this short guide is to explain what sustainability is and how you can introduce sustainability into your work.
