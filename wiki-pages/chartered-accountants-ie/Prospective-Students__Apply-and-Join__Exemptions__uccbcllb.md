# University College Cork UCC

## Exemption criteria - BCL Law and Business

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC4409 Corporate Financing             AC3302 Investment Appraisal             AC3304 Introduction to Asset Pricing and Valuation |
| Management Accounting | AC4100 Management Accounting: Planning and Control             AC2204 Introduction to Management Accounting             AC2205 Intermediate Management Accounting |
| Financial Accounting | AC4408 Financial Reporting 2              AC4407 Financial Reporting 1               AC1102 Financial Accounting |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | LW3345 Company Law 1             LW3346 Company Law 2             LW2254 Commercial Law             LW1106 Law of Torts 1             LW1104 Foundations of Legal System             LW1154 Contract Law |
