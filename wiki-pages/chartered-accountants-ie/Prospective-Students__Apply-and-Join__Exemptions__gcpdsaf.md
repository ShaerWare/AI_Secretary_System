# Griffith College

## Exemption criteria - Postgraduate Diploma of Science in Accounting and Finance Management

To claim exemptions from CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve an award of Postgraduate Diploma of Science in Accounting and Finance Management and a mark of not less than 50% in the modules set out below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Strategic Financial Management |
| Management Accounting | Strategic Performance Management |
| Financial Accounting | Financial Reporting |
| Taxation | Taxation (IRL) |
| Law for Accountants | Law and Governance |
