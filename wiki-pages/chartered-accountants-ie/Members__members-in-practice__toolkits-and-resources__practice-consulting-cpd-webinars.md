# Practice Consulting CPD webinars

Practice News April 2026 Edition- 17 April 2026
Join us for our April edition of Practice News, as we discuss the key issues relevant to small and medium sized practitioners. While the exact content of this session will be finalised closer to the air date, regular features such as recent updates in relation to UK & Irish Tax, as well as the latest Compliance & Technical news will be included.

Solicitors Accounts Regulations 2014 (NI) - 23 April 2026
Join the Practice Consulting Team and representatives of the Law Society of Northern Ireland as they focus on undertaking assurance engagements for your Solicitor clients based in Northern Ireland assessing their compliance with the Solicitors Accounts Regulations 2014. How best to use our new Institute Solicitors Accounts Regulations (NI) Work Programme will also be covered.

2026 Financial Reporting Essentials for Practice (ROI & NI) - 30 April 2026
During this half day webinar, the Practice Consulting Team are going to provide an overview of the key 2026 financial reporting issues to be mindful of for micro, small and medium sized companies using FRS 102 and FRS 105 in the UK and Ireland.

Taking Your Practice to the Next Level Virtual Workshop (ROI & NI)7 May 2026
Have you set up your own Practice or joined into an existing firm as a Partner over recent years? Maybe you are in practice for a number of years and are looking to grow your firm? If so, join us at this half day online workshop as we cover the key issues that will assist you to take it to the next level.

Staying out of Trouble-Must Know Compliance & Quality Management Tips for your Practice 28 May 2026
From Anti Money Laundering to ISQM, CPD and Investment Business, there is seemingly ever-increasing levels of compliance involved in both setting up and running your own practice. This half day session will provide you an overview of the key regulatory needs that you must address to ensure that both you and your practice meet your compliance requirements and achieve the best possible quality management standards.
