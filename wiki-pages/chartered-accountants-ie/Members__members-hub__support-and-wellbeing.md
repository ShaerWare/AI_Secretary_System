# Support and wellbeing

Get more from your membership by checking in with Thrive, which offers a range of resources to help support your wellbeing. Members can also lean on CA Support when facing personal, professional and financial challenges.

Wellbeing and good mental health are fundamental to your success, but they may demand extra care and attention in the ever-changing landscape post-COVID. Are you aware of the supports available to you as a member?

### Did you know?

CA Support and Thrive provide assistance to members, students and their families. As you might expect, CA Support helps where there is illness, unemployment, debt and mental health issues.
