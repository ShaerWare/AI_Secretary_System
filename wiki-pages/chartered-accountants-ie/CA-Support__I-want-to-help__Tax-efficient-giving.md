# Tax efficient giving

## Tax back in Republic of Ireland

Donations to a charity of €250 or more in one calendar year are tax efficient. By reclaiming tax on your donation we can increase it by an extra 45% at no extra cost to you. This is a valuable source of income for CA Support.

### How charity tax back works:

Following the 2013 changes to the charity tax back scheme, donations of €250 or more are now eligible for tax reclaim for both PAYE tax payers and the self-assessed.

That means that your donation of €250 is actually worth €362 to CA Support – at no extra cost to you.

The more you give, the more the Revenue gives. It really is that simple.

To enable CA Support to reclaim the tax, you must complete a Enduring Certificate and send it back to us. This form is valid for 5 years so if you make another tax efficient donation to us during this period, you don’t have to worry about completing another form for us.

If you prefer to complete a form for each year separately, you need to complete a Annual Certificate.

#### Companies

In the case of corporate donations, a company can claim a deduction as if it were a trading expense.

## Tax back in Northern Ireland

If you are a UK taxpayer, for every £1 you donate, CA Support will receive an additional 25p from HM Revenue & Customs. All you need to do is complete a Gift Aid Declaration. You only need to complete the declaration once, as it will cover all future donations you make. There is no minimum amount you need to donate – the only requirement is that you are a UK taxpayer. It’s that simple.

## Have you donated just under €250 this year?

If you have donated just under €250 this year you can grow your donation by 45% by calling +353 1 6377342 or by donating online.
