# Technological University of the Shannon: Midlands Midwest (formerly Limerick IT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) in Accounting & Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in key modules as set out below. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCT08019 Strategic Corporate Finance (Minimum 50%)             FINC08019 Financial Management Analysis (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06059 Management Accounting – Decision Making (Minimum 40%) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT07030 Advanced Management Accounting (Minimum 50%)             ACCT06063 Management Accounting – Planning & Control (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06059 Management Accounting – Decision Making (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06069 Cost Accounting – Fundamentals (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06068 Cost Accounting – Core (Minimum 40%) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT08024 Financial Reporting IFRS (Minimum 50%)             ACCT07023 Advanced Financial Accounting (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06057 Financial Accounting (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06053 Financial Accounting For Companies (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06049 Financial Accounting Fundamentals (Minimum 40%)             ACCT06047 Financial Accounting Principles (Minimum 40%) |
| Taxation | TAXE07015 Corporation Tax and Capital Gains Tax (Minimum 50%)             TAXE06010 Income Tax and VAT (Minimum 40%) |
| Law for Accountants | LAWE08060 Company Law 2 (Minimum 50%)             LAWE07034 Company Law 1 (Minimum 40%)             LAWE06082 Law and Business 2 (Minimum 40%)             LAWE06081 Law and Business 1 (Minimum 40%) |
