# ACCA

## Exemption criteria

ACCA members may be exempt from CAP1 and CAP2 of the Chartered Accountants Ireland programme.

Part-qualified students of ACCA who hold a degree from a recognised third level college or university may be eligible to claim exemptions from our CAP1 and CAP2 examinations on a paper for paper basis.

*Exemptions for ACCA are granted on a case-by-case basis.  Applicants must have secured a pass in the papers listed.
