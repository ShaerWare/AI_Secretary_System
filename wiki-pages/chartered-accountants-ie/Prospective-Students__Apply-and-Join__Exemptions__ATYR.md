# Accounting Technicians Ireland

## Exemption criteria - Second Year Examination

Candidates qualifying in the second year examination with ATI must achieve an overall grade of no less than "pass" to gain entry to our CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1) programme. Students must achieve the clear pass within a one year timeframe. Once the overall pass has been achieved candidates may be eligible for the following exemptions:

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Second year examination must be completed within a one year timeframe |
| Management Accounting | 50% in Management Accounting (Year 2) |
| Financial Accounting | 50% in Adv. Financial Accounting (Year 2) |
| Taxation | 50% in Adv. Taxation (Year 2) |
| Law for Accountants | 50% in Law & Ethics (Year 1) |
