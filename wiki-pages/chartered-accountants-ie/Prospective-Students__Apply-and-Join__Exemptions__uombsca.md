# Ulster University Derry-Londonderry

## Exemption criteria - BSc. (Hons) Accounting and Finance Part-time/Full-time/Higher Level Apprenticeship

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACF 358 Managerial Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACF148 Introduction to Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACF 537 Managing Financial Performance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACF 311 Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACF 540 Financial Accounting II (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACF 309 Financial Accounting I (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACF 109 Introduction to Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACF 325 UK Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW 309 Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
