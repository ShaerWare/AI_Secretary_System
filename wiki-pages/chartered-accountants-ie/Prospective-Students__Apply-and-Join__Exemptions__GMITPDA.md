# Atlantic Technological University (formerly GMIT)

## Exemption criteria - Postgraduate Diploma in Business in Accounting

To claim exemptions from CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve the award of Postgraduate Diploma in Business; and a mark of not less than 50% in the key modules set out below. Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCT09005 Financial Management (Minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT09004 Management Accounting (Minimum 50%) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT09003 Financial Accounting (Minimum 50%) |
| Taxation | ACCT09001 Taxation 1 (Minimum 50%)             ACCT09002 Taxation 2 (Minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | LAWL09001 Business Law (Minimum 50%)             LAWL09002 Corporate Law (Minimum 50%) |
