# Atlantic Technological University (formerly GMIT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) in Finance and Economics

To claim exemptions from CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must have achieved an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better; and, a clear pass, without compensation, in all relevant modules with a mark of not less than 50% in key modules as set out below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FINA08015 Financial Management 3 Corporate Finance (Minimum 50%)              FINA07020 Financial Management 2 Investment Decisions (Minimum 40%+)              FINA07019 Financial Management 1 Managerial Finance (Minimum 40%+) |
| Management Accounting | N/A |
| Financial Accounting | N/A |
| Taxation | ACCT07025 Irish Taxation Environment (Minimum 50%) |
| Law for Accountants | N/A |
