# Complaints about GB/NI Insolvency Practitioners

Chartered Accountants Ireland does not handle insolvency related complaints where the practitioner is operating under an insolvency licence in Great Britain or Northern Ireland. Such complaints should be submitted to the Insolvency Service’s Complaints Gateway. Contact details are set out below and further information is available on the Insolvency Service’s Website  (link below).
