# South East Technological University (formerly Carlow IT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) in Accounting and Finance

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all relevant subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the specified modules.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FNCE C4306 Advanced Corporate Finance 2 (Minimum 50%)              FNCE C4305 Advanced Corporate Finance 1 (Minimum 40%)              FNCE C3326 Corporate Finance in Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT C4309 Advanced Management Accounting (Minimum 50%)             ACCT C4306 Management Accounting 2 (Minimum 40%)             ACCT C3314 Management Accounting 1 (Minimum 40%)             ACCT C3312 Cost Accounting (Minimum 40%)             ACCT H2336 Management Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT C3313 Financial Reporting 2 (Minimum 50%)              ACCT C3311 Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum 40%)             ACCT H2337 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum 40%)             ACCT H1317 Financial Accounting (Minimum 40%) |
| Taxation | TAXS C4303 Capital Taxes and Succession (Minimum 50%)              TAXS C4302 Corporation Tax (Minimum 40%)             TAXS C3306 Business Tax for Self-Employed and VAT (Minimum 40%)             TAXS C3305 Income Tax (Minimum 40%) |
| Law for Accountants | LAWS C3312 Commercial/Corporate Law for Accountants 2 (Minimum 50%)              LAWS C3311 Commercial/Corporate Law for Accountants 1 (Minimum 40%)               LAWS H2333 Business Law (Minimum 40%) |
