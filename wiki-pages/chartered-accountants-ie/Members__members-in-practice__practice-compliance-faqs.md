- Where can I access all the practice toolkits I need for my practice? Practice Consulting have a suite of toolkits for all your practice needs.  You will find them in the Shop on our website in practice toolkits. Back to Top
- Where can I find the Public Practice Regulations? The Public Practice Regulations can be found on the Professional Standards section of the website, along with Guidance on Public Practice Regulations. Back to Top
- Where can I find out information on professional ethics? The Institute’s ethics resources can be found in the Ethics Resource Centre. Resources here include the Chartered Accountants Ireland Code of Ethics. Back to Top
- Where can I access information for training for the services I need for my practice? The Practice Consulting team can provide all your training needs through their various workshops. You can also visit CPD with whom we work closely to support our members training requirements. Back to Top
- Where can I find more information about Anti-Money Laundering (AML)? Please see the Professional Standards Department’s AML section of the website, including reporting obligations and guidance on the Money Laundering Supervision Regulations (ROI). Our popular Anti-Money Laundering Policies and Procedures practice toolkit is available in our online shop. Back to Top
- Where can I find out my General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)? Please see our dedicated GDPR resources section, including our GDPR resources for practices. Back to Top
- Our office is currently looking at moving from physical hardcopy to electronic file storage. Is there any requirement to hold the original signed accounts for clients, or are scanned copies are sufficient? While not aware of any legal requirement that would prohibit you simply maintaining a scanned electronic copy of such “wet” signed financial statements for your clients, our Institute advice would be to still hold a hard copy of these financial statements.
