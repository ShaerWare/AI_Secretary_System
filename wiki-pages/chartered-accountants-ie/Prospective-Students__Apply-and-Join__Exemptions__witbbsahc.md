# South East Technological University (formerly WIT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Hons) (Accounting Stream) via Level 7/Higher Certificate

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all relevant subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the specified modules.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Applied Finance (50%+)             Business Finance (40%+) |
| Management Accounting | Decision Making (50%+)             Management Accounting 2 (40%+)              Management Accounting 1 (40%+) |
| Financial Accounting | Advanced Financial Reporting (50%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 2 (40%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 1 (40%+)             Fundamentals of Accounting 2 (40%+)             Fundamentals of Accounting 1 (40%+) |
| Taxation | Taxation (50%+)             Income Tax (40%+) |
| Law for Accountants | N/A |
