# TaxSource Total

Chartered Accountants Ireland's searchable, complete online tax resource. No subscription required. Updated for Finance Act 2024.

- Irish tax legislation*, with new links to Revenue Tax and Duty Manuals

#### *Irish tax legislation includes

- Taxes Consolidation Act, 1997 (as amended up to and including Finance Act 2024)
- Stamp Duties Consolidation Act, 1999 (as amended up to and including Finance Act 2024)
- Capital Acquisitions Tax Consolidation Act 2003 (as amended up to and including Finance Act 2024)
- Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010 (as amended up to and including Finance Act 2024)
- Local Property Tax Act 2012 (as amended up to and including Finance Tax Appeals Act 2021)
- Finance (Tax Appeals) Act 2015
- Provisional Collection of Taxes Act, 1927
- Waiver of Certain Tax, Interest and Penalties Act, 1993
- Interpretation Act 2005
- Emergency Measures in the Public Interest (Covid-19) Act 2020 (as amended up to and including Finance Act 2022)
- Financial Provisions (Covid-19) (No. 2) Act 2020
