# Strategic leadership

The Practice Consulting team can help members and their management team to set strategic goals and action plans to achieve results within defined periods. This will prove to be a winning formula for Chartered Accountants who want to maximise the return on their financial and emotional investment in the business.

## Benefits

- Define your goals and compile action plans for the business and for each department with assigned responsibilities
- Set goals within a strategic context to ensure you can swiftly make decisions on challenges such as, how best to compete in the market, recruitment, succession planning, merging, purchasing fees and exiting the practice.
- Benchmark your practice against your peers
