#### Compliance Assignments

#### Audit and accounting files

The purpose of reviews carried out by us is educational, in order to assist you with your compliance obligations. The scope of the reviews of audit and accounting files is limited to procedures in relation to technical issues in the presentation of financial statements and the content and organisation of files, and as such we are not carrying out a second audit in the case of audit files. Nor are we making any comment on the truth or fairness or completeness of financial statements reviewed. The report does not constitute a reaffirmation of audit opinions issued and the issuing of appropriate audit opinions rests with the firm.  To the fullest extent permitted by law, we accept no liability for any costs or damages suffered by your firm in any way associated with reliance on any reports issued by us. We will not complete a taxation review and we make no comment on to the basis of the opinions insofar as these relate to the treatment of taxation in the financial statements.
