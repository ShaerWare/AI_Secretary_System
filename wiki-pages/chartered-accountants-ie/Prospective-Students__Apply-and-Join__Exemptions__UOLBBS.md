# University of Limerick

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business Studies

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum overall 2.ii grade in their degree.Candidates must achieve a clear pass in all relevant options as well as meet the individual subject criteria. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Managerial Accounting             Financial Information Analysis             Finance             Corporate Finance             (Minimum average QCA 2.5, any D grade takes average below exemption threshold) |
| Management Accounting | Managerial Accounting             Management Accounting 1             Management Accounting 2             (Minimum average QCA 2.5, any D grade takes average below exemption threshold) |
| Financial Accounting | Principles of Accounting             Financial Information Analysis             Financial Accounting and Reporting             Advanced Financial Reporting             (Minimum average QCA 2.5, any D grade takes average below exemption threshold) |
| Taxation | Tax Theory & Practice             Corporate Taxation             (Minimum average QCA 2.5, any D grade takes average below exemption threshold) |
| Law for Accountants | Legal Environment for Business             Company & Partnership Law             (Minimum average QCA 2.5, any D grade takes average below exemption threshold) |
