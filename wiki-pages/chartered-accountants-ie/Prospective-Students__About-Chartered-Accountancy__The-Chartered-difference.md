## Your global passport

- USA – The Irish Chartered Accountancy qualification is fully recognised by the  dominant accountancy body in the US (AICPA), one of the few finance and accountancy qualifications to have such valuable recognition.
- UK – 23% of members are based in UK or NI.
- Continental Europe – Chartered Accountants Ireland qualification has European recognition.
- Australia – 1,000 Irish Chartered Accountants work in Australia.
- New Zealand/Canada/Hong Kong/South Africa – Mutual Recognition Agreements.
- England, Wales and Scotland – Reciprocity Agreements.
