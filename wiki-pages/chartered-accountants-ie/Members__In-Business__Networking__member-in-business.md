# Special Interest (Focus) Groups

Under the auspices of the Members in Business Committee, Chartered Accountants Ireland offers a number of special interest or focus groups for members working in particular sectors. These fora allow members to discuss issues that arise in their day to day working environment and to asses future challenges and requirements. Groups occasionally run public seminars on topics of interest.

## Special Interest Groups

- Risk Management/Internal Audit
- Commercial State Enterprises Group
- Banking
- Insurance
- Public Sector Committee
- Charity & Not for Profit Group
- Interim Managers Interim Manager's Linkedin Group
- Pharma
- Mediators Forum

For further information about these groups or suggestions for further groups please contact MembersServices@charteredaccountants.ie
