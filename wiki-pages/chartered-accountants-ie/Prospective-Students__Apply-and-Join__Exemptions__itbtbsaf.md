# TU Dublin Tallaght/Blanchardstown Campus

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) in Accounting & Finance

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required with a mark of not less than 50% in the key modules outlined below. First cohort of graduates will be in 2025.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCF H4024 International Corporate Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCF H3024 Financial Management (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCF H4014 Advanced Management Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCF H4015 Advanced Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H2016 Management Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H2015 Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCF H4018 Group & Company Financial Reporting 1(Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCF H3023 International Financial Reporting 3 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H2018 International Financial Reporting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H2013 International Financial Reporting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H1021 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H1014 Financial Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACCF H4021 Advanced Corporate Taxes (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCF H4013 Advanced Personal Taxes (Minimum mark of 40% required)              ACCF H3013 Business & Capital Taxes (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H2014 Introduction to Taxation(Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | ACCF H2017 Company Law (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCF H1016 Business & Employment Law (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCF H1013 Principles of Law  (Minimum mark of 40% required) |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.i honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing and Assurance | ACCF H3015 Auditing & Assurance (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Strategic Finance and              Management Accounting | n/a |
| Financial Reporting | n/a |
| Taxation II ROI | n/a |
