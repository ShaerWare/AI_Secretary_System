# University of Dundee

## Exemption criteria - BA in Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Advanced Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Intermediate Financial Management (Minimum mark of 40% required)             International Business Environment (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | Advanced Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Intermediate Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Introduction to Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | Advanced Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Intermediate Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Introduction to Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | Tax II (or Fiscal Studies) (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Tax I (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
