# Maynooth University

## Exemption criteria - Higher Diploma in Professional Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve 50% in the required college options.  Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis from CA Proficiency 1.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC503 Finance for Accounting (Minimum of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | AC505 Management Accounting (Minimum of 50% required)              AC205 Management & Cost Accounting (Minimum of 50% required) |
| Financial Accounting | AC501 Financial Accounting (Minimum of 50% required)              AC151 Introduction to Accounting (Minimum of 50% required) |
| Taxation | AC207 Personal and Business Taxation (Minimum of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LW480 Law and Governance (Minimum of 50% required)              LW180 Business Law & Legal Process (Minimum of 50% required) |
