# Practice Link application form

Please fill out the form below if you would like to advertise your wish to buy, sell or merge a practice or block of fees.

The charge is €200 plus VAT (£170) to post your advertisement for six months. This can be renewed for a further six months upon payment of a second fee. An invoice is sent on receipt of the form and payable prior to the advertisment being posted on the website.  Read more in the terms and conditions.

All fields in the form are mandatory.

Description of practice/block of fees that will be published on website and Practice Matters newsletter:
Please provide a description of practice/block of fees for sale, merger or to purchase. This description will be published to the website/Practice Matters in full and will not be edited or amended by the Practice Consulting team. If you wish retain anonymity, do not include any identifying details below.

Please include the following information:

- General location of the area in which the firm is based.
- Description of what is for sale/merger or what you wish to purchase.
