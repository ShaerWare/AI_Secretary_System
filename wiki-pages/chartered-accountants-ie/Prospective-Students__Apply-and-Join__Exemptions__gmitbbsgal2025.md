# Atlantic Technological University (formerly GMIT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) Accounting (Galway) (3 year degree)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required with a mark of not less than 50% in the final year examination.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCT08055 Corporate Finance & Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             FINA07010 Financial Management 2 Investment Decisions (Minimum mark of  40% required)             FINA07016 Financial Management 1 Managerial Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT08055 Corporate Finance & Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT07046 Management Accounting 3 (Acct) (Minimum mark of 40% required)              ACCT07045 Management Accounting 2 (Acct) (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT06058 Management Accounting 1 (Acct) (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT08022 Corporate Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT07044 Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT06056 Financial Accounting (Acct) (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACCT08053 Taxation 2 (Acct) (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT07041 Taxation 1 (Acct) (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAWL08021 Corporate and Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
