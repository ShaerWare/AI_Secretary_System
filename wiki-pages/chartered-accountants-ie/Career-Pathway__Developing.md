# Level 2: Developing [3-10 years PQE]

This stage of your career allows you to cement the roads you have travelled so far and make faster and more focussed progress. Since qualification, you may have already taken on some further training and this can be a good time to do more, all consolidating your existing strengths. Check your progress and accomplishment of the skills using the pathway and get some recommendations and suggestions for where you can go next.
