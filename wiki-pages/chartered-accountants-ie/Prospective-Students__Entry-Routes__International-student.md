# I am an international student

*Please note, all students of Chartered Accountants Ireland must be based on the island of Ireland and be able to attend lectures or classroom based revision. As well as this, you must be able to work on the island in order to gain your necessary work experience. All exams must also be taken here. The Institute cannot advise on visa arrangements, please visit INIS website for more information on immigration requirements.

To study with Chartered Accountants Ireland, you must be a resident in Ireland to attend classes and workshops. In order to qualify students must pass the relevant exams and complete the required work experience and must be eligible to work in Ireland.

The Institute cannot advise on visa arrangements, you can find out more information about entry to Ireland here.

## Training options

## Training route: Training Contract

The Training Contract route is a structured programme that combines working and training. Traditionally employers will hire recent college or university graduates. The contract typically lasts 3.5 years. During the period of the contract the Chartered exams are taken and the relevant work experience is acquired in order to qualify.
