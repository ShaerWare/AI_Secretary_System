# Corporate A-Z: Arachas

## Risk & Insurance Solutions for Chartered Accountants Ireland members and member firms

Arachas provides Chartered Accountants Ireland members with specialist risk advisory support and insurance solutions. With over 35 years’ experience supporting accountancy professionals across the Republic of Ireland, Arachas understands the specific risks and exposures that their practices or firms face.

As Ireland’s largest nationwide insurance broker, with 14 offices across the country, Arachas has the expertise to support businesses of all sizes - from SMEs and growing practices to large multi-partner firms and corporate clients.

In addition to advising professional services firms, they provide specialist risk management support across a wide range of sectors including construction, retail, manufacturing, real estate, food and beverage and hospitality. The Corporate Practice team at Arachas combines deep sector knowledge with extensive market expertise, helping larger and growing companies manage complex risks and protect their businesses.

They provide a comprehensive range of services and products including:

- Professional Indemnity Insurance
- Office Combined Insurance
- Commercial and Corporate Insurance Solutions
- Expert risk management advice
- A dedicated Corporate Practice team

If you would like to discuss any of your business insurance requirements, please call Arachas on (01) 231 5000.

Chartered Accountants Ireland members can also access exclusive car, home and travel insurance schemes through Arachas’s Affinity Car & Home team. Call 045 247 305 to obtain a quotation.

## Employee Benefit Programmes for Chartered Accountants Ireland member firms

Arachas Employee Benefits (formerly Glennon Employee Benefits) provides independent advice and tailored employee benefit programmes to help member firms attract, retain and support their employees. With over 50 years’ experience, they help businesses of all sizes – from small firms to large multinationals - review and optimise benefits programmes to ensure they remain competitive and cost-effective. The team at Arachas Employee Benefits provides ongoing support and proactive engagement to help employers manage these programmes and communicate their value effectively to employees.

Services for employer members include:

- Group Pensions Scheme design, governance and ongoing support
- Group Life Assurance and Income Protection
- Group Medical, Dental and Wellness Plans
- Business Protection solutions such as Key Person and Shareholder Protection

Arachas Employee Benefits also provides personal financial protection and planning services for individual members, including:

- Mortgage Protection
- Life Assurance
- Income Protection
- Pension Planning & Advice
