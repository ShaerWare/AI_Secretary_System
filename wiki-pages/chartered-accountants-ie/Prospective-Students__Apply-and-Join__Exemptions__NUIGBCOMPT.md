# University of Galway

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor Degree in Commerce (Online)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. Candidates must meet the criteria and have a clear pass without compensation in the key module set out below. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AY8101 Business Finance |
| Management Accounting | Not Awarded |
| Financial Accounting | Not Awarded |
| Taxation | Not Awarded |
| Law | Not Awarded |
