# Technological University Dublin (DIT)

## Exemption criteria - BSc (Hons) in Accounting / BSc (Hons) in Accounting (International)

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all relevant subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the specified modules

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FNCE3004 Corporate Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             FIN2003 Finance 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             FIN2002 Finance 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT3005 Accounting for Management Control and Decision Making (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT9210 Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT3003 Advanced Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT9207 Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT9206 Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACCT3002 Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW1004 Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.i honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing & Assurance | ACCT3004 Auditing (Minimum 50%) |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | N/A |
| Financial Reporting | N/A |
| Taxation II | N/A |
