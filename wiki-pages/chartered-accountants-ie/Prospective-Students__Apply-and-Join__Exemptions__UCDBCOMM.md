# University College Dublin UCD

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Commerce / Commerce International / BSc Business

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of the criteria are available from the university.

* If a semester has been studied abroad, we will require a letter from UCD confirming the equivalent subjects were completed. (This letter is not relevant for UCD BComm (International) Graduates, as the business courses studied during the Academic Year Abroad, are not designated by UCD.)

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |

| Finance | FIN20010 Principles of Finance*             ACC20020 Management Accounting |
| Management Accounting | ACC20020 Management Accounting*             ACC30030 Cost Management |
| Financial Accounting | ACC10040 Financial Accounting 1             ACC20010 Financial Accounting 2             ACC30020 Financial Accounting 3 |
| Taxation | ACC30010 Tax in Society |
| Law for Accountants | LAW10400 Business Law 1: Transactions, Negotiations and Organisations              ACC30050 Corporate Governance, Company Law and Labour Law |
