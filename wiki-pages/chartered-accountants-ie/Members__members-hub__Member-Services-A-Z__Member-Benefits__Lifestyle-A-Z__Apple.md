# Lifestyle A-Z: Apple

## The Corporate Employee Purchase Program (CEPP) from Apple (pricing subject to change)

Apple CEPP allows our members to access preferred pricing on a wide range of Apple Products. All discounts are applied to the prices on the online store. There is no code required at checkout.

Apple Vision Pro is not available to order on CEPP stores.
