# Griffith College

## Exemption criteria - BA (Hons) Accounting & Finance

To claim exemptions from CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii honours or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in key modules indicated below. Details of the criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Corporate Finance (50%+)             Financial Management and Analysis (40%+) |
| Management Accounting | Performance Management (50%+)             Management Accounting (40%+) |
| Financial Accounting | International Corporate Reporting (50%+)             International Financial Reporting (40%+)             Financial Accounting and Analysis (40%+)             Financial Accounting (40%+) |
| Taxation | Corporation Taxation and Capital Gains Tax (Elective)             Income Tax and Vat                                        (Average mark of 50% across both modules, with no less than 40% in either) |
| Law for Accountants | Business and Company Law (50%+) |
