# Munster Technological University (formerly IT Tralee)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business Studies (Hons) Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Not awarded |
| Management Accounting | Not awarded |
| Financial Accounting | Regulatory Framework (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Accounting & Finance (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Financial Accounting  (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Company Law  (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Applied Corporate Law  (Minimum mark of 50% required)             Business Law  (Minimum mark of 40% required)             Introduction to Irish/European Law  (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
