# Atlantic Technological University (formerly IT Sligo)

## Exemption criteria - BA Hons Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree.  A mark of 50% in all relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Candidates must have secured an average of 50% over both semesters  (provided the options are split in the same academic year).  Candidates must not score less than 40% in either semester in order to avail of the average.   Details of the criteria are available from the college.
