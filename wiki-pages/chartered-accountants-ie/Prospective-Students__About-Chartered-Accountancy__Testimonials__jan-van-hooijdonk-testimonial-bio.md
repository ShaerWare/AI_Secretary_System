# Jan Van Hooijdonk, Tennants Textile Colours Ltd

### Tell us about your academic background?

I studied Design Engineering before completing a Masters in Mechanical Engineering at Queen’s University Belfast.

### Why accountancy?

I decided about 10 years ago that engineering wasn’t for me and that I wanted a career change. I was always interested in business and was good with numbers so I went for accountancy and haven’t looked back since.

### Why Chartered Accountants Ireland?

The ACA qualification seems the prime accountancy qualification, it opens the most doors.

### Can you explain the Flexible Route to someone who doesn't know what it is?

You can take exams at your own pace and building professional experience as you work.

### Describe a typical day for you?

No such thing as a typical day - I am a Financial Controller and completing the course has enabled me to take on a varied job.

### Is it flexible?

Yes - you can increase or decrease your pace of study as required.

### How was combining work and study?

A big commitment but worth it in the end. A supporting family and a study group help.

### Can you see how someone who is not an accountant might benefit from this qualification?

Yes - it helped me progress further in an accountancy career than I ever went in engineering.

### Would you recommend the Flexible Route to others? Why?

Yes, it’s a big commitment but worth it to further your career. ACA is the most prestigious accountancy qualification.

### What advice would you give someone who is considering the Flexible Route?

Visit an information event/open evening or ring up the Institute and go for it.

### What success are you looking for? / What are your future goals?

To progress in my accountancy career and make it to Finance Director or CFO somewhere.
