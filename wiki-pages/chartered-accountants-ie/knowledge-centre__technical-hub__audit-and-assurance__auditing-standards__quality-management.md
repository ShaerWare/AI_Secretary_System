# Quality management standards

Quality Management

In 2022, a major shift took place in how audit and assurance firms manage quality. Three international standards came into effect:

- ISQM 1
- ISQM 2
- ISA 220 (Revised)

These standards introduced a risk-based approach to quality management. Now, every audit firm must operate a System of Quality Management (SoQM) tailored to its size, structure, and services.

| IAASA Standards relevant to Ireland | FRC Standards relevant to UK |
| --- | --- |
| International Standard on Quality Management (Ireland) 1, (Updated September 2024) | International Standard on Quality Management (UK) 1 (Updated September 2025) |
| International Standard on Quality Management (Ireland) 2 (Updated October 2024) | International Standard on Quality Management (UK)2 (Updated September 2025 |
| International Standard on Auditing (Ireland) 220 Quality management for an audit of financial statements (Updated October 2024) | International Standard on Auditing (UK) 220 Quality management for an audit of financial statements (updated September 2025) |

What Does This Mean for Your Firm?

The standards require firms to customise their quality management systems based on their unique circumstances. Most firms have now embedded these standards into their methodologies and have undergone internal and external reviews. The guidance and tips on the attached webpage may be useful for you assessing your progress and ongoing compliance with the standards.
