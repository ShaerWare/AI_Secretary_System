# Lifestyle A-Z: Circle K

## Circle K EV fuel card

Circle K are delighted to announce we have recently launched a Circle K EV Fuel Card which can be simply added to your existing Fuel Card Account.

We can also arrange a stand alone EV account if needed.  Our Circle K EV Card can be used for EV & Car Wash Only.

We will continue to operate the existing 3 cent per litre off pump price offer for Diesel & Unleaded at all Circle K locations nationwide along with 30% off Car Wash at our Company Owned Locations (this offer does not work at Circle K Express sites).

You will be invoiced each Monday for the previous week’s transactions

Our Circle K EV Fuel Card works at various EV locations including Circle K Locations along with many others nationwide including ESB, Ionity & e-Power charging points.

Connector Types on Circle K Chargers

Supported Connectors:

- All Circle K high-speed charging stations in Ireland provide CCS (Combo 2) CHAdeMO

Compatibility:

- CCS is used by the majority of modern EVs, while the CHAdeMO plug accommodates models like the Nissan Leaf and other Japanese EVs – ensuring every EV driver can charge at Circle K.

Power per Connector:

- Circle K’s CCS connectors support high-power DC charging (typically 100 kW up to 150–300 kW 50–60 kW max

- Our forecourts offer convenience facilities – you can park and charge amenities (restrooms, coffee, food)

100% Renewable Energy:

- All electricity 100% renewable sources

Using the Charger – Payment (“Tap & Go”)

Simple Payment Process:

- Circle K’s fast chargers are pay-as-you-go select the Credit/Debit Card option on the screen and tap your card

“Tap to Start, Tap to Stop”:

- Our Alpitronic chargers feature convenient contactless payment. Tapping your card same card again no RFID fobs or accounts needed

No Cables Needed:

- Every Circle K fast charger has integrated charging cables do not need to bring their own cable

Pricing:

- EV charging at Circle K is charged per kWh of energy delivered.
