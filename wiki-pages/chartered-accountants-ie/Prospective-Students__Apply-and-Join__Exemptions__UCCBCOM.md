# University College Cork UCC

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Commerce

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet the criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC3302 Investment Appraisal             AC3303 Introduction to Asset Pricing & Valuation             AC4409 Corporate Financing |
| Management Accounting | AC2204 Introduction to Management Accounting             AC2205 Intermediate Management Accounting             AC4100 Management Accounting: Planning & Control |
| Financial Accounting | AC1100 Introduction to Accounting             AC4407 Financial Reporting 1             AC4408 Financial Reporting 2 |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | LW1108 Introduction to Legal System             LW1109 Introduction to Business Law             LW3300 Company Law |
