# University of Limerick

## Exemption criteria - MSc in Accounting / Postgraduate Diploma in Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve the award of the Masters / Postgraduate Diploma in Accounting; and a grade of C1 or above (without compensation) in each module as set out below. Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis. The required experience for qualification will be dependent upon the number of exemptions secured. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing and Assurance | AC6151 Auditing, Assurance and Governance  (minimum C1)             AC6152 Advanced Auditing and Assurance (minimum C1) |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | IN6131 Risk Modelling and Derivative Markets  (minimum C1)             AC6112 Strategic Financial Performance and Management Accounting (minimum C1) |
| Financial Reporting | AC6161 Advanced Financial Reporting I (minimum C1)             AC6122 Advanced Financial Reporting II (minimum C1) |
| Taxation II (ROI) | TX6141 Advanced Taxation I (minimum C1)             TX6132 Advanced Taxation II (minimum C1) |
