# University College Cork UCC

## Exemption criteria - BSc in Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in the relevant subjects is required and candidates must meet criteria for individual subject exemptions. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AC1105 Investment Analysis             AC1106 Introduction to Asset Valuation             AC2121 Corporate Financial Policies |
| Management Accounting | AC1109 Management Accounting: Principles & Concepts             AC1115 Management Accounting: Cost & Control Systems             AC4100 Management Accounting; Planning & Control |
| Financial Accounting | AC1103 Financial Accounting Fundamentals             AC1104 Accounting for Partnerships and Companies             AC2100 International Financial Reporting 1             AC2101 Consolidated Financial Statements and Reporting |
| Taxation | AC2115 Introduction to Taxation             AC4007 Taxation: Income Tax & VAT |
| Law for Accountants | Not awarded |
