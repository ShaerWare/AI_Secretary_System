## Regulatory decisions 2022

- Withdrawal of a firm's audit registration














    (?) 04/03/2025 14:54:20














    (?) The Institute of Chartered Accountants in Ireland regulates its members in accordance with the provisions of its Bye Laws openly and in the public interest. Oversight of this role is performed by the Professional Standards Board. The Institute hereby places notice that:
Withdrawal of a firm’s audit registration
The Quality Assurance Committee made an order to withdraw the audit registration of the firm Toner McDowell, of The Mount, 21 Woodstock Link, Belfast, BT6 8DD for failing to comply with the requirements of the Audit Regulations. This order took effect on 19 December 2022. Withdrawal of a firm's audit registration
