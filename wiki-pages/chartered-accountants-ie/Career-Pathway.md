# Let us help you get there

Your career is your journey and we are with you every step of the way. Whether you are planning your next steps, are at a crossroads hoping to change direction or want to continue with the road you're on, we have created this Career Pathway tool to help you to see clearly the road ahead and mark the important touchstones you have already reached. This map will help you navigate the way with suggestions and guidance relevant to your career stage. Read on to see how you can assess your skills, goals and aspirations. Enjoy the journey.

## Where are you now?

## Level 1: Young professional [0-3 years PQE]

## Level 1: Young professional [0-3 years PQE]

You are exploring the first avenues in your career. You have qualified and are developing your knowledge and confidence. You may know exactly what roads to travel, or you might want some advice and steering.

## Level 2: Developing [3-10 years PQE]

## Level 2: Developing [3-10 years PQE]

You have been qualified for up to 10 years so are building experience in key skills areas. You have the opportunity now to really specialise and get ahead; there are many paths to choose from and it can be daunting so keep using our support.

## Level 3: Expert [FCA]

## Level 3: Expert [FCA]

More than 10 years qualified, you have developed real expertise and are becoming more and more skilled. You may now yourself be guiding others along the way. There may be fewer obvious choices and the "what next" question might be harder to answer. We're here to help.

## Level 4: Leader [C Suite CEO/CFO]

## Level 4: Leader [C Suite CEO/CFO]

You have reached a pinnacle and may question how this does not become a plateau. Your technical skills may be less relevant now as your decision-making and judgement come to the fore; choosing a path may be more challenging.
