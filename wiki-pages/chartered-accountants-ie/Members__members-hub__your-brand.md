# Your brand

As a member of Chartered Accountants Ireland, you are part of a connected network and community of professionals. Make the most of your membership and show the world that you are a Chartered Accountant.

International research indicates that showcasing your designation appeals to businesses using and employing Chartered Accountants. It demonstrates that you are part of a network and you have the training, expertise and experience needed to help support local and global communities, businesses and economies now and into the future. Following the steps below will help highlight that you are part of the Chartered Community.

## Tips for showcasing your professional brand as a Chartered Accountant

### Using the Institute branding

- Make sure you use the ACA/CPA or FCA/FCPA designation after your name on email signatures, business cards, stationery, website bios/content, and your LinkedIn profile
- Where appropriate, use the Chartered Accountants Ireland logo
- Take advantage of branded Teams and Zoom backgrounds (download below)

### Making the most of LinkedIn

- Ensure you have listed Chartered Accountants Ireland under the Education
- If you are involved with on a committee, board, or special interest group, make sure you list this under Volunteering
- Invest in a professional headshot/photo for your LinkedIn profile picture, website bios etc.
- Follow the Institute's LinkedIn page
- Update your LinkedIn profile background with a branded image showcasing the Chartered Accountants Ireland brand or Chartered Accountant Worldwide member network logo (download below)
