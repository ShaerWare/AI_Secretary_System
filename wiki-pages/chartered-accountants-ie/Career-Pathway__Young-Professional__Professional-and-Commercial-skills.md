Professional & commercial skills

## What do they look like?

You are building a wide range of skills at this stage in your career that you will need to focus on equally, including ethics, diplomacy, resource management and more.

Professional skills include commercial awareness, self-management, and a recognition of the culture of the organisation and broader business, economic and political contexts.

### Professional values

- Possess professional values including integrity, impartiality, honesty, objectivity, respect and confidentiality
- Show a commitment to quality, continuous improvement and accountability for decisions and actions
- Continually acquires and apply new knowledge and learning to improve performance
- Raise compliance, ethical or other issues to protect the organisation's reputation and obligations - Show your commitment to the public interest
- Exercise professional scepticism and judgement

### Resilience

- Capacity to recover quickly from setbacks
- Put matters in perspective and do not take criticism personally
- Demonstrate a positive level of stamina and determination when dealing with obstacles
- Be able to recognise the triggers and impacts of stress and then implement techniques to deal with it

### Interpersonal effectiveness

- Able to communicate cross functionally within an organisation
- Listen and read cues from various sources to assess how to adjust and respond appropriately
- Actively identify and nurture formal and informal contacts that will facilitate the achievement of strategic goals

### Organisational knowledge

- Understand organisational structures and the dynamics of the operations
- Demonstrate awareness of how your area contributes to the organisation
- Be aware of the needs of the organisation and the internal and external factors impacting the business

### Team work

- Ability to work as part of a team; working collaboratively with others to achieve goals
- Resolve issues that arise with minimal direction
- Take new initiatives aimed at improving team performance

### Communication skills

- Can write on complex and specialised issues
- Convey critical financial information to facilitate complete understanding of financial matters

### Diplomatic sensitivity

- Maintain objectivity when your own positions or opinions are challenged
- Encourage others to contribute by overcoming diversity issues and promote inclusion

### Develop talent

- Encourage the development of more junior team members
- Provide constructive feedback and seek feedback for your own personal development

### Career management

- Invest time in considering and planning for the next stage of your own career. Seek professional support with the development of a career plan

### Resource management

- You manage your own work schedule, set and meet deadlines and involve stakeholders

### Focussing on achievement

- Set challenging goals for yourself and your area of responsibility

### Networking

- Able to develop networks and to manage professional relationships

## Development activities

#### How can you get there?

Here are our suggestions for boosting your skills in the three main areas at this stage of your career.
Don't forget, many of these are FREE!

- Use this Career Pathway template to set goals, improve performance and identify gaps
- Use the coach and mentoring services
- Develop a career/personal development plan
- Keep a reflective learning journal
- Review performance appraisal feedback
- Take some more CPD courses
- Keep reading industry publications like Accountancy Ireland
- Read The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People
- Refer to Harvard Business Review Forbes articles
- Volunteer for social committee and events
- Get involved with sports teams or community groups
- Review TedTalks
- Develop your LinkedIn
- Take on some exercise/yoga/mindfulness
- Get involved in key projects within your organisation
- Build your network
- Explore the area of emotional intelligence
