# Level 1: Young professional [0-3 years PQE]

You are recently qualified, are learning the essentials of the workplace in what might be your first "real" job. This is your chance to learn, observe, try new things, push out your boundaries. Have a look at the skills areas below to see how you can set, articulate and achieve your career goals at this important stage.

## Which skill do you want to learn more about?

## L1: Leadership skills

## L1: Leadership skills

At this stage, being a strong team member is as important as leading. Observe others, network and try a variety of projects to build confidence.

## T1: Technical skills

## T1: Technical skills

At this point you are focussing on honing the skills you learned during your training; you may be exploring options to expand these.

## PC1: Professional & commercial skills

## PC1: Professional & commercial skills

You are building a wide range of skills at this stage in your career that you will need to focus on equally, including ethics, diplomacy, resource management and more.
