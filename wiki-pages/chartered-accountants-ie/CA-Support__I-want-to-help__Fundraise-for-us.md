# Fundraise for us

Without generous people like you, we wouldn’t be able to help those within our profession who have fallen on hard times. There are endless ways you can fundraise for CA Support. Check out some of our tips below.

#### Coffee Morning

Coffee mornings are a great way to get everyone together for a nice cuppa and a slice of delicious cake. Setting one up is easy. You can host it at your home and invite your friends and family over. Alternatively you can choose a local venue and invite the public to attract as many people as possible.

You can ask friends and family to help you with the organisation. There may be some amongst you with a hidden talent for baking! You can either charge an entry fee or charge people per coffee and cake.

Contact our CA Support Fundraising and Marketing Executive to receive your fundraising pack.

#### Cycle

Are you a cycling enthusiast in search of a challenge? You can participate in events such as Great Dublin Bike Ride, Ring of Kerry or Tour de Connemara in aid of CA Support.

Remember you need to register your participation with the event organisers directly.

Contact our CA Support Fundraising and Marketing Executive to receive your fundraising pack.

#### Marathons, fun runs and triathlons

Why not get the adrenaline flowing and sign up to one of the many marathons, fun runs and triathlons across Ireland, and raise vital funds for CA Support in the process. Among the countless options available are the Women’s Mini-Marathon, Dublin Marathon, Cork City Marathon, Belfast City Marathon, Dingle Marathon, Triathlone, Triathy and Tough Mudder.

Remember you need to register your participation with the event organisers directly.

Contact our CA Support Fundraising and Marketing Executive to receive your fundraising pack.

#### Get creative

Your fundraising event can whatever you want it to be. You can organise a table quiz, climb Kilimanjaro, abseil down the largest building in town, shave your head or organise a five-a-side football game. The choice is endless and you can be as imaginative as you like.

Simply register your event with us by contacting and we will support you with it. We have posters, balloons, stickers, sponsorship forms, t-shirts and collection buckets to help you with your event.

#### Create your online fundraising page

Maximise your fundraising efforts by creating your own online fundraising page using one of the following fundraising websites:

- Just Giving
- iDonate

Just email your friends the link to the page or share it through social media and they can support you by donating via credit or debit card. The money raised will be then securely transferred to CA Support’s account. It couldn’t be easier or safer!
