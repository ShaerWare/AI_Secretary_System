# Webinars and conferences

## Responsible AI: What businesses need to know (February 2025)

A webinar from February 2025 about Responsible AI and what businesses and their advisors need to know about how to navigate the complexities of balancing compliance with innovation, risks with benefits.

You can view this on-demand webinar on the CPD shop page.

The slides from the Responsible AI webinar are also available to download (PDF, 970KB).

## Technology Conference 2025 (January 2025)

Recorded in January 2025, the Technology Conference showcased the transformative impact artificial intelligence has on the accountancy profession.

You can access this on-demand conference on the CPD shop page.

## AI in the workplace (October 2024)

A webinar from October 2024 about artificial intelligence in the workplace. Made in conjunction with the Ulster Society and A&L Goodbody, Aisling Byrne, Partner, gave her professional insight into the regulation of AI, the use of AI in the workplace, key risks and effective compliance.

You can view this webinar on YouTube.

The slides from the AI in the workplace webinar are also available to download (PDF, 1,577KB).

## Inspiring excellence with Nigel Botterill (December 2023)

In this webinar, Nigel Botterill talks about the lessons he has learned and his experience of business transformation and AI.

You can view this webinar about inspiring excellence on YouTube.
