# Contact us

Our team is here to answer your questions. You can contact us using the form, or you will find contact information for relevant teams further down the page.

Our hours of operation are 9am to 5.30pm, Monday to Friday. We aim to answer your query as soon as possible. However, it may take our teams a little longer during peak periods such as exams and enrolments in August – October, or during member renewals from December – March.

If you are a student or member, including your student/member number on the form will help us answer your query more quickly.

All information provided on this form is routed to the relevant team and is used only for the purpose of answering your query.
