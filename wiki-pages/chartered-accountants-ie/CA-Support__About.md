# About us

Life can throw us all kinds of challenges. We don’t always know what’s around the corner. Redundancy, bereavement, depression, illness and family crisis can happen to any one of us, when we least expect it. CA Support is here to offer financial support to Chartered Accountants, Accounting Technicians, students and their family members for life.

Should you find yourself in a difficult financial situation, Chartered Accountants Support (CA Support) will help you through any adversity.
