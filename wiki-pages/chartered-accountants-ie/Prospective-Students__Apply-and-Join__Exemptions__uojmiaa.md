# Ulster University Derry-Londonderry

## Exemption criteria - MSc/PgDip International Accounting with Analytics

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve an overall pass or better (i.e. the award of the Masters degree or the Postgraduate Diploma); and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each subject as set out below. Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis. The required experience for qualification will be dependent upon the number of exemptions secured. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Audit & Assurance | ACF758 International Audit and Assurance (minimum 50%)             ACF757 Strategic Leadership for International Accounting (minimum 50%) |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | ACF761 Strategic Finance (minimum 50%)             ACF762 Strategic Management Accounting (minimum 50%) |
| Financial Reporting | ACF760 International Financial Reporting (minimum 50%)             ACF764 International Corporate Reporting and Tax Planning (minimum 50%) |
| Taxation II (NI) | ACF759 Advanced UK Taxation (minimum 50%)             ACF764 International Corporate Reporting and Tax Planning (minimum 50%) |
