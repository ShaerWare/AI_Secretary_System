# South East Technological University (formerly WIT)

## Exemption criteria - BA (Honours) in Accounting

#### CAP1/CAP2

In a new initiative we are happy to inform you that as well as CAP1 exemptions this programme now qualifies for additional exemptions from CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2). To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), graduates require an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, have a clear pass, without compensation in all relevant subjects of the degree with a mark of not less than 50% in the specified modules.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Applied Finance (50%+)             Business Finance (40%+) |
| Management Accounting | Intermediate Management Accounting 1 (50%+)             Management Accounting Techniques (40%+)             Costing (40%+) |
| Financial Accounting | Advanced Financial Accounting (50%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 2 (40%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 1 (40%+)             Introduction to Financial Accounting 2 (40%+)             Introduction to Financial Accounting 1 (40%+) |
| Taxation | Income Tax (40%+)             Taxation (50%+) |
| Law for Accountants | Company Law 2 for Accountants (50%+)             Company Law 1 for Accountants (40%+)             Law for Accountants (40%+) |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.i honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Audit and Assurance | Auditing 1 (50%)             Auditing 2 (50%) |
| Strategic Finance and              Management Accounting | N/A |
| Financial Reporting | N/A |
| Taxation II (ROI) | N/A |
