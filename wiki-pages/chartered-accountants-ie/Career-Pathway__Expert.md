# Level 3: Expert [FCA]

You have a wealth of experience and a lot to offer. You are probably established in your career and gaining more mastery of your role. Now your focus may be moving to consolidating your position and to formalise your learning through qualifications. Through this experience and your further training and development, you actively add value to your team and organisation. Check your progress on the Career Pathway and see what suggestions and recommendations we have for your next steps.

## L3: Leadership skills

## T3: Technical skills

## PC3: Professional & commercial skills
