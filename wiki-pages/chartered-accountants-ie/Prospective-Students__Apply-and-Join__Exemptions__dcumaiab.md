# Dublin City University DCU

## Exemption criteria -MSc in International Accounting & Business

Candidates wishing to secure exemptions at CAP1 level, must secure a pass mark of 50% (without compensation) in the following relevant subjects in order to secure the exemptions listed below.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FBA1002 Financial Management (Minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | ACC1025 Management Accounting: Costing, Planning and Control (Minimum 50%)             ACC1030 Management Accounting: Decision Making and Performance (Minimum 50%) |
| Financial Accounting | ACC1023 International Financial Accounting Principles (Minimum 50%)             ACC1024 International Financial Reporting (Minimum 50%) |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Not awarded |
