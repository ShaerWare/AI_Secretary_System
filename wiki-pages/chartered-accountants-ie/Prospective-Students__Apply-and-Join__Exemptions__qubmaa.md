# Queens University Belfast

## Exemption criteria - Master of Advanced Accounting with Placement / Master of Advanced Accounting

CAP2
To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve the award Master of Advanced Accounting with Placement / Master of Advanced Accounting; and a mark of 50% or above (without compensation) in each module as set out below. Exemptions are awarded on a subject-by-subject basis. The required experience for qualification will be dependent upon the number of exemptions secured. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Auditing and Assurance | ACC3009 Auditing and Accountability (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC4001 Financial Reporting and Auditing (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Strategic Finance and Management Accounting | ACC3011 Advanced Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC4003 Financial Management (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Financial Reporting | ACC2004 Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC3010 Advanced Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC4001 Financial Reporting and Auditing (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Taxation II (NI) | ACC3012 Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACC4002 Advanced Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
