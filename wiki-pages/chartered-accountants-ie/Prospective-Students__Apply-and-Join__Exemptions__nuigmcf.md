# University of Galway

## Exemption criteria - MSc Corporate Finance

The following exemption arrangement is applicable to graduates  who have achieved an overall minimum honours level of 2.ii or better, with a mark of not less than 50% in the relevant modules identified in the template.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | AY5109 Advanced Corporate Finance (Minimum 50%)             AY5108 Intermediate Corporate Finance (Minimum 50%) |
| Management Accounting | AY5141 Accounting and Management Control for Sustainable Organisations (Minimum 50%)             AY5106 Accounting for Management (Minimum 50%) |
| Financial Accounting | AY5122 Advanced International Financial Reporting and Analysis (Minimum 50%)             AY5131 International Financial Reporting & Analysis (Minimum 50%) |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Not awarded |
