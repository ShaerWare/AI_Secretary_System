# South East Technological University (formerly WIT)

## Exemption criteria - Bachelor of Business (Honours) Accounting Stream

#### CAP1/CAP2

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Applied Finance (50%+)             Business Finance (40%+) |
| Management Accounting | Decision Making (50%+)             Management Accounting Techniques (40%+)             Costing (40%+) |
| Financial Accounting | Advanced Financial Reporting (50%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 2 (40%+)             Intermediate Financial Reporting 1 (40%+)             Introduction to Financial Accounting 2 (40%+)             Introduction to Financial Accounting 1 (40%+) |
| Taxation | Taxation (50%+)             Income Tax (40%+) |
| Law for Accountants | Company Law 2 (50%+)             Company Law 1 (40%+)             Business Law (40%+)             Introduction to Business Law (40%+) |

#### CAP2

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 2 (CAP2), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.i honours degree and a mark of not less than 50% (without compensation) in each module as set out below.

| CAP 2 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Audit and Assurance | Auditing 1 (50%)             Auditing 2 (50%) |
| Strategic Finance and              Management Accounting | N/A |
| Financial Reporting | N/A |
| Taxation II (ROI) | N/A |
