# Coaching and recruitment

Get more from your membership by availing of career guidance and coaching with your Institute.  Wherever your journey takes you, we'll help you take the next step.

The expert Careers team at Chartered Accountants Ireland helps members identify opportunities to grow as professionals, no matter where they are in the world or in their career. Get support with your job search, one-to-one interview preparation, and personalised career advice including guidance on reaching your individual goals.

### Did you know?

You can avail of a career coaching session as part of your membership. Coaching includes comprehensive interview preparation support, and is conducted by a dedicated careers and recruitment specialist with a deep understanding of the profession and the specific career options for Chartered Accountants across industries.
