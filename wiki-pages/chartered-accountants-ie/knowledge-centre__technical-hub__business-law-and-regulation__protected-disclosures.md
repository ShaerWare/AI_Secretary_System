# Protected disclosures/whistleblowing

## What is whistleblowing?

Whistleblowing, speaking up,"bell ringing" (as it's known in the Netherlands) is a wide term. The resources on these pages focus on the making of a protected disclosure and in particular protected disclosures  legislation and guidance.

Whistleblowing is more formally known as ‘making a protected disclosure’. The law protects persons who raise concerns about possible wrongdoing in the workplace. They are also protected if they are dismissed or penalised for reporting a possible wrongdoing.

In the following pages we provide information and resources to members on matters such as:

- the applicable legislation,
- who is protected,
- what kind of disclosure of information is protected,
- what do firms have to do to comply with whistleblowing legislation,
- when do they have to do it,
- reporting obligations,
- confidentiality,
- data protection considerations.
