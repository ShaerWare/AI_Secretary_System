# Technological University Dublin (DIT)

## Exemption criteria - BSc Accounting via Higher Certificate in Business & Finance

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | FNCE3004 Corporate Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required)             FIN2003 Finance 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             FNCE2018 Business Finance B (Minimum mark of 40% required)             FNCE2017 Business Finance A  (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT3005 Accounting for MA & Decision Making (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT9210 Management Accounting 1 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT3003 Advanced Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT9207 Financial Reporting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT2001 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACCT3002 Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW1004 Business Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
