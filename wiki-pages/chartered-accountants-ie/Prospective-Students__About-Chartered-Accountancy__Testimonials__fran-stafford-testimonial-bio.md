# Fran Stafford, Bank of Ireland

### Tell us about your academic background?

- Degree - BSc BIS in UCC
- Masters - MSc Strategy & People Mgt in NUIG

### Why accountancy?

I have always loved accounting even though my degree was 50% IT, I was always more drawn to the accountancy and business subjects.

### Why Chartered Accountants Ireland?

ACA is such a respected and widely recognized qualification, I wanted to make sure that I was gaining a strong enhancement for my CV and getting a great return for investing my time, money and effort.

### Can you explain the Flexible Route to someone who doesn't know what it is?

The Flexible Route is set up to allow you qualify as a Chartered Accountant while working outside of the traditional training contract model.

### Where have you worked and what kind of roles have you been in?

I worked in Bank of Ireland for 13 years. Starting on the grad programme, I progressed through a variety of roles in Corporate Banking including relationship & portfolio management, restructuring and my current role is new business origination.

### Describe a typical day for you?

My role is new business origination so I am out and about for a lot of coffees and meetings to invest in and grow my network. I originate new customers / business for Corporate Banking and have responsibility for managing each transaction / opportunity through all stages of our process - info gathering & analysis, development of proposal and structure, negotiations with customers, completion of credit application and presentation to credit committee, legal documentation, to final drawdown.

### Is it flexible?

Yes the access to lectures online was key for me in making it flexible as I often could not make it to lectures so really important to be able to catch up whenever works in your schedule.

### How was combining work and study?

Difficult, it’s very important to have a supportive manager & employer.

### Can you see how someone who is not an accountant might benefit from this qualification?

Yes, broad reach of subjects which are all relevant in a business context.

### Would you recommend the Flexible Route to others? Why?

Yes, it’s a great way to attain the ACA qualification and you bring a different perspective when you haven’t come from the traditional training contract route.

### What advice would you give someone who is considering the Flexible Route?

Ideally make sure you have full support from your employer/manager before you undertake it, important they commit to giving you the time for study and understanding when it comes to assignment / continuous assessment deadlines. Set out for them at the start of the year what your study and exam schedule is like, when you will need study leave, etc.

### What success are you looking for?

To continue progressing my career in Corporate Banking by taking on more strategic roles and additional responsibility within the new business team.

### What are your future goals?

Continue to have a fulfilling career but maintain a healthy work life balance. I hope to take up further studies when my kids are a little older!
