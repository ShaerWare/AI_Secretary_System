# Governance

CA Support is registered as a charity in Ireland and Northern Ireland. CA Support complies with all relevant legislation and with the Charities Regulator Governance Code. The overall governance structure of CA Support is set out in the Governance Handbook.

## Our team

CA Support is governed by a Board of Trustees supported by a team of staff. Find about more about us here.

## Annual report and financial statements

Chartered Accountants Support DAC is registered with the Charities Regulatory Authority in Ireland, in compliance with the Charities Act 2009 and has voluntarily adopted the Statement of Recommended Practice (SORP) for accounting and reporting by charities. Chartered Accountants Support DAC is independently audited by BDO.

Chartered Accountants Support Ltd is registered with the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland. It has voluntarily adopted the Statement of Recommended Practice (SORP) for accounting and reporting by charities. Chartered Accountants Support Ltd is independently audited by leading audit and assurance practice BDO.
