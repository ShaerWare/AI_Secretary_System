# Ulster University Belfast

## Exemption criteria - BSc in Accounting and Law

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the noted examinations. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACF306 Personal & Business Finance (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACF322 Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACF131 Principles of Management Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACF318 Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACF117 Principles of Financial Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACF112 Introductory Accounting (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | ACF564 Taxation (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW559 Company Law (Minimum mark of 50% required)             LAW334 Business & Commercial Law (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
