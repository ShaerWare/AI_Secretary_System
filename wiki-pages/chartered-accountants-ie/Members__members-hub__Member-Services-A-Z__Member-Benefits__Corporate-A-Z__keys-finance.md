# Corporate A-Z: Keys Finance

## Next-Day Professional Fee Finance for Chartered Accountants Ireland members

### About Keys Finance

With offices in both Belfast and London, Keys Finance have been supporting accountancy practices across the UK and Ireland with Next-Day Fee Finance since 2006, and have built up a team of highly skilled finance experts who are always on hand to support and discuss opportunities for your practice.  Our business is client-driven and we focus on working in partnerships, to deliver the right answers.  We can do this by remaining independent, which allows us to deliver exceptional first-class service with personality.

What sets us apart from other fee finance providers, is our Next-Day Professional Fee Finance product, which is a versatile and convenient way to reduce practice debtor days and credit control administration whilst at the same time boosting cashflow for you and your client. There is no need to wait 10+ days to receive your fees when you can get paid next-day with Keys!

### Benefits of Keys Finance

- Receive 100% of your fees with Next-Day Professional Fee Finance.
- You will have a dedicated account manager and named head office point of contact ensuring we deliver the right answers and provide an exceptional level of service.
- Provide clients with a flexible repayment plan between 2-12 months.
- No minimum usage requirement - meaning you can use the service as little, or as often as you want.
- Rolling credit agreement – no need for additional forms at fee renewal.
- No client arrangement fees.

### Next-Day Professional Fee Finance vs Invoice Factoring

Late Payments are estimated to affect 58% of B2B invoices in the Republic of Ireland, putting businesses under considerable cashflow pressure. It has also been noted 62.4% of all invoices in Northern Irelands SME Sector were paid late.

Next-Day Professional Fee Finance provided by Keys Finance improves on this by providing further valuable solutions to address cash flow challenges.
