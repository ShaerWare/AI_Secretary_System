# Technological University of the Shannon: Midlands Midwest (formerly Athlone IT)

## Exemption criteria - BA Accounting

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve a minimum of a 2.ii honours degree. A clear pass without compensation in all relevant subjects is required as well as a mark of not less than 50% in the final examination in each of the subject areas. Details of criteria are available from the college.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | ACCT 07026 Financial Management 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Management Accounting | ACCT 08017 Advanced Management Accounting (or Management Accounting 3) (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT 07025 Management Accounting & Decision Making (or Management Accounting 2) (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT 06032 Management Accounting 1A (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT 06033 Management Accounting 1B (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Financial Accounting | ACCT 08008 Corporate Reporting (Minimum mark of 50% required)             ACCT 07006 Financial Accounting 2 (Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT 06047 Financial Accounting 1A ((Minimum mark of 40% required)             ACCT 06046 Financial Accounting 1B ((Minimum mark of 40% required) |
| Taxation | TAX 08004 Business Tax (or Taxation 2) (Minimum mark of 50% required) |
| Law for Accountants | LAW 06022 Corporate Law 1 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             LAW 07008 Corporate Law 2 (Minimum mark of 50% required)             LAW 06017 Business Law (Minimum mark of 40% required) |
