# Lifestyle A-Z: Ulster Society Private Medical Scheme

## Ulster Society Private Medical Insurance Scheme

Members of the Ulster Society are welcome to join our private medical scheme offered by AXA Health, a leading private medical insurance provider, recommended by our advisers, Towergate Employee Benefits.

Towergate Employee Benefits are happy to advise you with regards to the AXA Health policy and whether it meets your personal demands and needs. They will also discuss alternative insurers with you if you need this option.

If you have a policy elsewhere or are considering taking out a new policy

Towergate Employee Benefits are an independent intermediary and can advise on a range of options across the insurance market for both individual members and company paid policies. To find out more please read the details on the following link:

https://www.towergatehealthandprotection.co.uk/perch/resources/towergate-everybody-benefits-one-pager.pdf

## How to avail of the offer

Contact Towergate Employee Benefits by telephone on 0800 3897724, Monday – Friday 9am -5pm or by email on haps-charteredaccountantsie@towergate.co.uk
