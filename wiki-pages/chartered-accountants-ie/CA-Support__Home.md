# What is CA Support?

CA Support is a registered charity which offers practical and financial support to Chartered Accountants, Accounting Technicians, students, and their family members for life. Should you find yourself in a difficult financial situation, Chartered Accountants Support (CA Support) can provide a helping hand to support you through life’s ups and downs.
