# University College Cork UCC

## Exemption criteria - MSc Accounting & Performance Analytics (previously MSc Management Information & Managerial Accounting Systems)

To claim exemptions from any part of CA Proficiency 1 (CAP1), candidates must achieve an overall pass or better (i.e. the award of the MSc) and a mark of 50% or higher in all relevant subjects of the MSc as set out below. Details of criteria are available from the university.

| CAP 1 subjects | College/university options required |
| --- | --- |
| Finance | Not awarded |
| Management Accounting | AC6419 Principles of Cost Management             AC6420 Advanced Managerial Accounting and Governance             AC6421 Performance Management, Risk and Control for International Firms |
| Financial Accounting | AC6002 Accounting Information Fundamentals             AC6424 Financial Reporting 1             AC6425 Financial Reporting 2 |
| Taxation | Not awarded |
| Law for Accountants | Not awarded |
