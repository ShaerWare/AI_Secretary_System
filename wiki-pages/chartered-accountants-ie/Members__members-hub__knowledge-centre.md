# Knowledge Centre

The Institute's Knowledge Centre is a valuable repository of core professional topics - no matter what issue matters to you, you'll find all the detail you need in the Knowledge Centre.

Visit the hub to check out the Institute journal, Accountancy Ireland, published bimonthly. The Knowledge Centre also hosts content from specialised teams across the Institute, creating practical guides and up-to-the minute reports on issues that make a difference to accountants.

As Brexit progressed, our Tax team steered members (in both the EU and UK) through ever-changing customs regulations, all hosted in the Knowledge Centre. As COP27 unfolds, our Sustainability team is bringing you the latest news on what it means for ESG reporting and the road ahead in terms of public policy. Members in practice can find the latest toolkits and resources, maintained by the Practice Consulting team.
