# Presentations

## Public Sector Blitz

On 21 March the Ulster Society in partnership with Grant Thornton hosted a Public Sector Blitz in Belfast designed for accountants, as well as other business professionals, working in, or advising, the public sector.

Speakers included former Head of NICS David Sterling; Rodney Allen, NI Audit Office; Gina McIntyre, SEUPB; Claire Thomson, Grant Thornton; and Roisin Loughran, Grant Thornton.

This event was oversubscribed and so we recorded this event for those who were unable to attend, this recording can be viewed below.
