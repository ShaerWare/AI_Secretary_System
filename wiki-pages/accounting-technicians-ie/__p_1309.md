# 21st January: Join ATI’s Virtual Open Day

ATI is delighted to host our January 2026 Virtual Open Day, designed to help prospective learners explore the practical, flexible, and career-focused study routes available for the year ahead.

Whether you’re starting your career, returning to education, or looking to gain a recognised qualification to enhance your professional prospects, this online event will guide you through everything ATI has to offer.

## What to Expect During the Virtual Open Day

### Learn About the ATI Qualification

Participants will receive a clear overview of the Accounting Technician qualification, including what the programme involves, the subjects covered, and the professional skills you will develop. You’ll learn how the ATI pathway provides a strong foundation in accounting, finance, and business, preparing you for a wide range of career opportunities.

### Explore All Three January Study Routes

1. ApprenticeshipIdeal for those who want to earn while they learn, this two-year work-based programme combines academic study with practical, on-the-job training. You’ll work with an approved employer while completing your ATI modules, gaining valuable real-world experience as you qualify.

2. OnlinePerfect for learners who need maximum flexibility. The Online route allows you to study from anywhere, with structured online classes, digital learning resources, and dedicated tutor support, enabling you to progress at a pace that fits your lifestyle and commitments.

## Who Should Attend?

This virtual event is ideal for:

- School leavers considering a practical alternative to full-time university
- Adults returning to education
- Career changers seeking a recognised qualification with strong employment prospects
- Anyone interested in building solid accounting and business skills

## Why Attend?

By joining the ATI January 2026 Virtual Open Day, you will:

- Understand the structure and benefits of the Accounting Technician qualification
- Compare all three study routes to find the option that suits your goals
- Learn how ATI supports students from enrolment through to qualification
- Receive clear guidance on next steps and application procedures
