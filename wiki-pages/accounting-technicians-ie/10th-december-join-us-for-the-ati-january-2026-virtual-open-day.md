# 10th December: Join Us for the ATI January 2026 Virtual Open Day

ATI is delighted to host our January 2026 Virtual Open Day, designed to help prospective learners explore the practical, flexible and career-focused study routes available for the upcoming term.

Whether you’re starting your career, returning to education, or seeking a recognised qualification to boost your professional opportunities, this online event will guide you through all the options ATI provides.

What to Expect During the Virtual Open Day

Learn About the ATI Qualification

Participants will gain a clear overview of the Accounting Technician qualification: What the programme involves, the subjects covered, and the professional skills you will develop. You’ll learn how the ATI pathway offers a strong foundation in accounting, finance, and business, giving you the confidence and competence to work in a wide range of roles.

Explore All Three January Study Routes:

1. Apprenticeship

Ideal for those who want to earn while they learn, this two-year work-based programme blends practical on-the-job training with academic study. You’ll work with an approved  employer while completing your ATI modules, gaining valuable, real-world experience as you train.

2. Online

Perfect for learners seeking maximum flexibility, the Online allows you to study from anywhere. With structured online classes, digital learning resources, and tutor support, you can progress through the programme at a pace that suits your lifestyle and commitments.

3. Part-Time Through a Partner College

If you prefer a classroom environment with face-to-face interaction, the Part-Time route delivered through our partner colleges across the country may be the right fit. This option offers blended learning, scheduled classes, and a supportive community of fellow students and experienced tutors.

Who Should Attend?

This virtual event is ideal for:

- School leavers considering a practical alternative to full-time university
- Adults returning to education
- Career changers seeking a recognised qualification with strong employment prospects
- Anyone interested in gaining solid accounting and business skills

Why Attend?

By joining the ATI January 2026 Virtual Open Day, you will:

- Understand the structure and benefits of the Accounting Technician qualification
- Compare all three study pathways to find the option that best fits your goals
- Learn how ATI supports students from enrolment through to qualification
- Get clear guidance on next steps and application procedures
