# 23 January Accounting Technician Apprenticeship Information Session

## Launch Your Future in Accounting – Apply for the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship Today!

Are you ready to turn your passion for numbers into a rewarding and future proof career? The Accounting Technician Apprenticeship, offered by Accounting Technicians Ireland (ATI), provides an exciting opportunity to earn a salary, gain hands on experience and achieve a nationally recognised qualification all at the same time. With applications now open for the January 2026 intake, now is the perfect moment to take the first step toward a successful career in accounting.

### A Smart and Structured Path Into Accounting

The Accounting Technician Apprenticeship is a two year programme that combines paid employment with college based learning. Developed by ATI, this pathway allows you to apply your learning directly in the workplace, giving you both the knowledge and practical experience needed to thrive in the accounting profession.

As part of the programme, you will work in a full time accounting role while attending college one day per week. This blended learning model provides a strong balance of technical training and hands on application, something that employers across Ireland value greatly.

### Why Choose the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship

The apprenticeship is more than a training programme. It is a direct route into a respected, stable and well paid career. As an apprentice, you will:

- Earn While You Learn
- Gain Real World Experience
- Qualify With ATI
- Develop In Demand Skills

This flexible and accessible programme is an excellent fit for school leavers, career changers or anyone who wants a practical and supported route into accounting. No previous experience is required, only motivation and a willingness to learn.

### Join a Supportive and Growing Community

When you join the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship, you become part of a strong community of learners, mentors, employers and educators who support your development every step of the way. ATI apprentices benefit from a structured learning environment, valuable professional contacts and a clear pathway for future career progression or further study.
