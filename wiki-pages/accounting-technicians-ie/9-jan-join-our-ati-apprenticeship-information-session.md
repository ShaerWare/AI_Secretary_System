### What Is the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship?

The Accounting Technician Apprenticeship is a two-year, work-based programme that combines paid employment with structured academic learning. Designed by ATI in collaboration with industry, the programme equips learners with the technical knowledge and practical skills required to succeed in accounting roles across industry, practice, and the public sector.

As an apprentice, you will work in a full-time accounting role while attending college one day per week. This blended approach allows you to apply what you learn in real time, building confidence and competence from day one.

### Why Choose the ATI Accounting Technician Apprenticeship?

This apprenticeship is more than a qualification — it is a direct pathway into a respected and in-demand profession. As an apprentice, you will:

- Earn while you learn €27,593 per year
- Gain hands-on experience
- Qualify with ATI
- Develop in-demand skills
- Build a strong foundation for long-term career progression or further study

No previous accounting experience is required — just motivation, commitment, and an interest in developing practical business and finance skills.
