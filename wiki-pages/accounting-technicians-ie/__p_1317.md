# ATI is hosting TWO Employer Information Sessions Next Week

As the finance and accounting landscape continues to evolve, organisations across Ireland are facing increasing challenges when it comes to attracting, developing and retaining skilled talent. Accounting Technicians Ireland (ATI) is delighted to invite employers from industry, practice and the public sector to attend our upcoming TWO Employer Information Session on the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship, taking place on Tuesday, 3 February AND Thursday 5 February BOTH from 12:00pm to 1:00pm.

This one-hour online session is designed specifically for employers who are seeking practical, sustainable solutions to build future-ready finance teams. Whether you are looking to recruit new talent, upskill existing staff, or plan for long-term workforce development, this event will provide valuable insight into how the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship can support your organisation.

## A Smarter Way to Build Accounting Talent

The Accounting Technician Apprenticeship is an innovative, two-year, work-based education programme that combines structured on-the-job learning with college-based study. Apprentices are employed from day one, enabling them to contribute directly to your organisation while developing strong technical, professional and workplace skills.

This blended approach ensures that learning is immediately applied in the workplace. Apprentices gain hands-on experience while studying accounting, taxation, law and business modules that reflect real-world practice. The result is capable, confident professionals who understand your business and add value from the outset.

## Why Attend the Employer Information Session?

During the session, Accounting Technicians Ireland will provide a comprehensive overview of the programme and outline what employers can expect when participating in the January 2026 intake. Topics covered will include:

- How the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship works in practice
- Employer responsibilities and supports available throughout the programme
- Funding options and employer grants
- Salary structures and progression pathways
- How apprentices integrate learning with day-to-day business operations

Attendees will also have the opportunity to ask questions and gain insight into how employers across industry, practice and the public sector are already benefiting from the programme.

## Key Benefits for Employers

Employers who engage with the Accounting Technician Apprenticeship can benefit from:

- Recruiting new talent or upskilling existing staff
- Access to a €2,000 annual employer grant per apprentice
- A structured starting salary of €27,593
- Highly motivated apprentices
- Improved staff retention and succession planning

This programme allows employers to build a skilled workforce while contributing to the development of the accountancy profession in Ireland.

## Be Part of a Nationwide Initiative

Employers across Ireland are already shaping the future of accountancy through this dynamic apprenticeship programme. By participating, your organisation becomes part of a nationwide initiative that is modernising accounting education and creating clear, accessible career pathways.
