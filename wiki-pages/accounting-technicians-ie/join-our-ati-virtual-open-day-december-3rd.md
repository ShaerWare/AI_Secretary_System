Whether you are taking your first steps into the world of accounting, returning to education after time away, or looking to enhance your professional profile with a recognised qualification, this event will provide a clear, comprehensive insight into what ATI has to offer.

Our Virtual Open Day is an ideal opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of how the Accounting Technician qualification can open doors to meaningful, stable, and rewarding career paths. # What to Expect During the Virtual Open Day

## Learn About the ATI Qualification

During the session, participants will receive an in-depth overview of the Accounting Technician programme. We will explore the structure of the qualification, the subjects you will study across your modules, and the essential professional skills you will develop along the way. You’ll discover how the ATI pathway builds a strong foundation in areas such as accounting, finance, business operations, taxation, and payroll—skills that are highly valued across industries. This qualification is designed to give you both the knowledge and confidence to pursue roles in finance departments, accountancy practices, industry, or the public sector.

### Explore All Three January Study Routes

To help you choose the study option that best suits your lifestyle, commitments, and learning style, we will walk you through the three study routes available from January 2026:

#### 1. Apprenticeship Route

The Accounting Technician Apprenticeship is a fantastic choice for learners who want to earn while they learn. This two-year programme combines academic study with structured workplace experience. As an apprentice, you will work with an approved employer, gaining hands-on experience in real accounting tasks while completing your ATI modules. This route is ideal if you prefer learning by doing and want to build professional experience as you train.

#### 2. Online Route

The Online route offers maximum flexibility for learners who need to balance their studies with work, family, or other commitments. You can learn from anywhere, accessing live online classes, recorded sessions, digital learning materials, and ongoing tutor support. This option allows you to study at a pace and schedule that suits your lifestyle, without compromising on academic quality or engagement.

#### 3. Part-Time Through a Partner College

If you thrive in a classroom environment and value face-to-face interaction, the Part-Time route through our partner colleges across the country may be the perfect fit. This option offers scheduled classes, blended learning opportunities, and the support of experienced tutors. You’ll also benefit from being part of a local learning community, making it easier to stay motivated and connected.
