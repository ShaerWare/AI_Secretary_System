# Hire an Accounting Technician Apprentice by October 9 and get a €2k employers’ grant

Employers – the registration deadline for the September intake of our Accounting Technician Apprenticeship is October 9 – a €2,000 employers’ grant is made for every apprentice hired.

Accounting Technician Apprentices embark on a two-year, earn-as-you-learn programme, where they typically work four days a week in your office, and study one day a week at a local Partner College.

Tuition is delivered by our Partner Colleges in North and South Dublin, Cork, Limerick, Galway, Mayo, Meath, Carlow, Monaghan and Waterford.

All tuition fees are fully funded, graduates qualify with a QQI Level 6 Advanced Certificate in Accounting, and our MIATI designation.

They’ll put their learnings into practice every day, and become a valued member of your accounting and finance teams.

You can choose to register an existing employee on the programme, or interview the high-calibre candidates that ATI has to offer.

Simply express your interest via the form below, and we’ll revert with further details and support your registration on the programme.

### Register your interest

A quick reminder of some of the benefits of taking on an Accounting Technician Apprentice:

1. Access to work-ready talent
Accounting Technician Apprentices are motivated learners who will train in real-world accounting and business tasks. They are ready to contribute, learn, and grow within your team.

2. State support
Tuition is fully funded by SOLAS, and employers who take on an apprentice can avail of a €2,000 annual grant per apprentice, – it’s practical support that makes investing in talent even more cost-effective.

3. ATI support
ATI works closely with employers, education providers, and training coordinators to support every stage of the apprenticeship. From mentoring resources to regular check-ins, we’re with you every step of the way.
