# Join us for one of our last Online Information Sessions for the September 2025 Intake

Want to fast-track your career in accounting? Learn how you can become a qualified Accounting Technician and start building your future at this free online event, on September 24, from 1pm to 1.45pm.

It’s the perfect opportunity to find out everything you need to know about the ATI qualification, what it involves, and where it can take you.

##### Register here

What to Expect

Our Online Open Day will take you through the Accounting Technician qualification, and how it can help you achieve your career goals.

The session includes:

- Information on the wide range of career opportunities Membership of ATI
- An outline of the course structure, entry requirements, fees, and opportunities to progress your career
- Guidance on how to apply and register, including details on funding support
- A live Q&A

Whether you’re a school leaver, a mature student, changing careers, or looking to upskill, this session is open to everyone interested in a career in accounting.

Why Choose Accounting Technicians Ireland?

The ATI qualification offers a practical, affordable, and respected route into the world of accounting and finance.

Our graduates work across all sectors – from large firms to small businesses, public sector roles, and self-employment.

With ATI, you can:

- Build a successful career in accounting, with a recognised qualification that can be gained in as little as two years
- Work while you study, with flexible learning options
- Use your ATI qualification as a stepping stone to further study or professional accountancy bodies
- Start earning while building real-world experience through our apprenticeship option
